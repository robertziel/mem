### Meta (Facebook) Behavioral Interview — Move Fast & Bold Values

**Meta core values (current as of mid-2020s):**

| Value | One-liner |
|---|---|
| **Move Fast** | Bias toward action, iterative delivery, ship and learn |
| **Be Bold** | Take risks, innovate, challenge the status quo |
| **Be Open** | Transparent communication; share information freely |
| **Build Social Value** | Impact on people and communities |
| **Focus on Long-Term Impact** | Sustainable decisions, not just quick wins |
| **Live in the Future** (newer addition) | Build for what's coming, not just what's here |
| **Meta, Metamates, Me** | Loyalty order: company → teammates → self |

> The 2022 internal "operating principle" set added emphasis on building toward the future and accountability to the company first.

**What Meta evaluates:**

| Trait | Signal interviewers look for |
|---|---|
| **Initiative + ownership** | Did you do it without being asked? |
| **Speed of execution** | How fast did you ship? |
| **Pragmatic risk-taking** | Did you bet on something with imperfect info? |
| **Learning from failure** | Did you recover and ship the next version quickly? |
| **Impact at scale** | Measurable result, ideally millions of users |
| **Open communication** | Giving and receiving tough feedback directly |
| **Cross-team collaboration** | Working across product / eng / data / design |
| **Strategic alignment** | Connecting your work to Meta's bigger bets |

**Common question categories with example prompts:**

| Category | Sample prompts |
|---|---|
| **Initiative / Move Fast** | "Tell me about something you built that wasn't asked of you." • "Describe a time you shipped something quickly. What tradeoffs did you make?" • "Tell me about a time you proactively identified and fixed a problem." |
| **Boldness / Risk** | "Describe a time you took a risk on a technical decision." • "Tell me about a time you challenged a process or approach." • "How do you decide when to ship vs polish further?" |
| **Openness / Feedback** | "Tell me about a time you gave tough feedback." • "How did you respond to critical feedback?" • "How do you ensure knowledge sharing?" |
| **Failure / Recovery** | "Describe a time you failed. How did you recover?" • "Tell me about a project that didn't go as planned." • "Time you launched something that didn't work." |
| **Impact** | "Most impactful project you've worked on. How did you measure impact?" • "Time you improved a system that affected many users." |
| **Cross-functional** | "Worked with PMs and designers under deadline pressure." • "Influenced a partner team to change direction." |
| **Long-term thinking** | "Time you traded short-term speed for long-term durability." • "Built something that scaled beyond original assumptions." |

**Tailoring stories — Meta-specific emphasis:**

| Default phrasing | Meta-tuned phrasing |
|---|---|
| "We worked on it for 3 months and shipped" | "I shipped the **first version in 4 days**, iterated daily based on feedback, and the final version landed in 3 weeks" |
| "We noticed the issue and fixed it" | "I noticed the issue **without being asked**, proposed a fix, and shipped it within the sprint" |
| "We improved the system" | "We **reduced p95 latency by 40%**, impacting 2M daily active users" |
| "The first attempt didn't work" | "The first approach failed in production. I rolled back in **15 minutes**, root-caused, and shipped a second version that succeeded — total 1-week recovery" |
| "We shipped on time" | "We shipped 2 weeks ahead of schedule by cutting non-critical scope; that decision unblocked another team's launch" |
| "We had a meeting about it" | "I wrote a **2-page memo** with the trade-offs and shared it openly. The discussion converged in one async thread" |

**Phrases that build credibility at Meta:**

| Phrase | Signals |
|---|---|
| "I shipped the first version in [days]" | Move fast |
| "I made the call to **launch and iterate** rather than wait for perfection" | Bold + move fast |
| "I noticed the problem and **proposed a fix without being asked**" | Initiative |
| "We **rolled back in N minutes**, learned, shipped v2" | Resilience + speed |
| "The data showed the experiment didn't move the metric — we killed it" | Pragmatic, data-driven |
| "I shared the **early prototype openly** to gather feedback" | Openness |
| "I wrote the post-mortem **the same day**" | Speed + learning |
| "It impacted **N million users**" | Scale |
| "I worked with PM and design from day one" | Cross-functional |

**Phrases that hurt at Meta:**

| Phrase | Reads as |
|---|---|
| "We spent 6 months gathering requirements" | Slow |
| "I waited for direction from my manager" | Lacks initiative |
| "I asked for permission before making the change" | Lacks boldness (vs Netflix's "lacks autonomy") |
| "We followed our standard process" | Process-bound, not move-fast |
| "We built it perfect on the first try" | Suspicious — Meta values iteration |
| "We avoided risk" | Anti-bold |
| "I was just executing what was decided" | No ownership |
| "We didn't talk about the problem because it was sensitive" | Anti-open |
| "I worked alone on this for months" | Anti-collaborative |

**The "ship-and-iterate" narrative arc:**

| Beat | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Identified opportunity | "I noticed [problem] in [signal]" |
| 2. Made fast first-cut | "I shipped a v1 in N days" |
| 3. Measured impact | "Initial data showed X" |
| 4. Iterated | "I shipped v2 with [improvements] within N more days" |
| 5. Quantified end state | "Final outcome: [metric] moved by Y%, [N users] affected" |
| 6. Learned | "Key learning: [generalizable principle]" |

**Move Fast and Don't Break Things (the modern version):**

> The original "move fast and break things" was retired around 2014; the current framing emphasizes **moving fast with stable infrastructure**. Stories that show you balanced speed AND reliability resonate.

| Anti-pattern | Better framing |
|---|---|
| "We moved fast and broke prod" | "We moved fast **with feature flags + canary**, shipped daily, and rolled back instantly when needed" |
| "We chose speed over quality" | "We split the work into shippable chunks; each chunk had its own test bar" |
| "We skipped review to ship" | "We shipped behind a flag with monitoring; review continued in parallel" |

**Boldness — what Meta means:**

| Type of boldness | Example |
|---|---|
| **Technical** | "I rewrote the recommendation system because the old one wouldn't scale to next year's traffic" |
| **Strategic** | "I argued against shipping a popular feature because it didn't fit our long-term direction" |
| **Cross-team** | "I proposed deprecating another team's API to reduce surface area company-wide" |
| **Process** | "I challenged a design-review requirement that was slowing us down without catching real bugs" |

**Failure stories — how Meta wants them framed:**

| Beat | Sample |
|---|---|
| Bold initial bet | "I led an effort to migrate 5 services to a new framework in one quarter" |
| What went wrong | "Two services hit unexpected runtime issues; we rolled back one, paused another" |
| Quick recovery | "Within a week, I had the path to fix the issue" |
| Concrete learning | "I now spike on the riskiest dependency before committing to a date" |
| Outcome | "Three of five migrated successfully; we shipped the remaining two next quarter with the new approach" |

> **Recovery speed and learning are what Meta evaluates** — not whether you avoided failure.

**Impact at scale — what counts:**

| Metric | Examples |
|---|---|
| User reach | "Affected 50M DAU" |
| Engagement | "Increased session length by 8%" |
| Speed | "Reduced TTI by 200ms across the app" |
| Cost | "Saved $X / month in infrastructure" |
| Quality | "Reduced production incidents by 40%" |
| Reliability | "Improved availability from 99.5% to 99.95%" |
| Adoption | "Adopted by 12 product teams within 6 weeks" |
| Ads / monetization | "Drove $XM uplift in revenue" |

**Cross-functional collaboration stories — required:**

| Stakeholder | What to mention |
|---|---|
| PM | Worked from day one to define scope; pushed back when scope drifted |
| Design | Iterated together; shipped shared design tokens or components |
| Data Science | Used experiments to drive decisions; called experiments cleanly |
| Marketing / GTM | Coordinated launch comms |
| Privacy / legal / policy | Engaged early on sensitive features |
| Security / IR | Hardened before launch; ran red-team exercises |

**Strategic-thinking questions — be ready:**

| Question | What to know |
|---|---|
| "What's Meta's strategic moat?" | Network effects, ad targeting at scale, infrastructure leverage |
| "Biggest risk Meta faces?" | Privacy regulation, ad-tracking shifts (ATT), competition for attention (TikTok), AI cost |
| "How does Meta make money?" | ~98% advertising; Reality Labs investment offsetting future bets |
| "If you ran [Family of Apps / Reality Labs / Ads], what would you change?" | Show structured thinking, not platitudes |
| "What's a Meta product decision you'd push back on?" | Demonstrates courage + business understanding |

**Anti-patterns Meta interviewers flag:**

| Anti-pattern | Reads as |
|---|---|
| Slow narratives ("we discussed for 3 months") | Anti-move-fast |
| No initiative — manager-driven stories | Lacks boldness |
| No quantified impact | Hand-wavy |
| Sole-author hero stories | Lacks collaboration |
| Avoiding risk / failure stories | Lacks growth |
| Trash-talking previous employer | Lacks openness done well |
| "I just executed the plan" | No ownership |
| "We did exactly what we planned" | No iteration |

**Meta-specific things to mention if relevant:**

| Concept | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Bootcamp** | New-hire experience — eng rotation through teams |
| **Phabricator → Workplace + GitHub** | Code review and collaboration tools |
| **A/B testing culture** | Almost everything is gated by experiment |
| **Internal-first launch** | Dogfooding within company before external |
| **PSC (Performance, Stack-Ranked Cycle)** | Twice-yearly performance review |
| **Open posting** | Internal job board; lateral mobility expected |
| **Family of Apps + Reality Labs** | Two business halves; very different cultures |

**Pre-interview checklist:**

| Item | Pass? |
|---|---|
| 8–10 stories with quantified impact | ✅ |
| At least 3 stories show speed of execution | ✅ |
| At least 1 bold-risk story with both win + recovery | ✅ |
| At least 1 cross-functional collaboration story | ✅ |
| At least 1 hard-feedback (gave or received) story | ✅ |
| At least 1 long-term-thinking story | ✅ |
| Strategic understanding of Meta's business | ✅ |
| 3–5 strategic questions for the interviewer | ✅ |

**Rule of thumb:** Meta values **builders who move fast, take initiative, measure impact, and recover quickly when things break**. Every story should have a **quantified result** and ideally a **fast first-cut + iteration arc**. **"I shipped first, then iterated"** beats **"I planned for months."** Show you can move at startup speed with the discipline of a public-company SRE.
