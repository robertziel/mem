### Well-Child Care — Milestones and Immunizations

**Visits schedule (US):**

| Age | Visits |
|-----|--------|
| Newborn | Day 3-5, 1 mo |
| Infancy | 2, 4, 6, 9, 12 mo |
| Toddler | 15, 18, 24, 30 mo |
| Childhood | Annual after 3 yr |

**Major developmental milestones (CDC 2022 update — "75% of kids" benchmarks):**

| Age | Gross motor | Fine motor / cognitive | Language | Social |
|-----|-------------|----------------------|----------|--------|
| 2 mo | Lift head briefly | Watch faces | Coo | Smile |
| 4 mo | Hold head steady, push up on arms | Reach for toy | Laugh, sounds | Smile spontaneously |
| 6 mo | Roll, sit with support | Mouth objects | Babble | Recognize familiar |
| 9 mo | Sit unsupported, crawl | Pincer emerging | "Mama/dada" non-specific | Stranger anxiety |
| 12 mo | Stand alone, cruise | Pincer grasp | First word | Wave bye-bye |
| 15 mo | Walk | Stack 2 cubes | Few words | Show / give object |
| 18 mo | Walk well, climb | Stack 4 cubes | 10-25 words; point | Pretend play |
| 2 yr | Run, kick ball | Stack 6 cubes; remove clothes | 50+ words, 2-word phrases (50% intelligible) | Parallel play |
| 3 yr | Tricycle, jump | Copy circle, draw line | 3-word sentences (75% intelligible) | Take turns |
| 4 yr | Hop on 1 foot | Copy cross | Tells stories; 100% intelligible | Cooperative play |
| 5 yr | Skip | Copy square / triangle, write some letters | Future tense; counts to 10 | Rule-following games |

**Red flags (refer):**

| Age | Concern |
|-----|---------|
| 2 mo | No social smile |
| 4 mo | Doesn't lift head |
| 6 mo | Doesn't reach for objects |
| 9 mo | No babbling |
| 12 mo | No pointing, no first word |
| 18 mo | No two-word phrases (or any words) |
| 2 yr | No two-word phrases; loss of any milestone |
| Any age | Loss of skill (regression) |
| Any age | Persistent primitive reflexes after 6 mo, or asymmetric movement |

**Autism screening:** **M-CHAT-R/F** at 18 and 24 mo.

**Live vaccines (avoid in immunocompromised, pregnancy):** MMR, varicella, rotavirus, intranasal flu (FluMist), yellow fever, BCG.

**Catch-up schedule** — see CDC catch-up schedule; minimum intervals matter.

**Anticipatory guidance highlights:**

| Age | Topics |
|-----|--------|
| Infant | Sleep position (supine), no honey <1y, breast/formula, vitamin D, car seat rear-facing, smoke-free |
| Toddler | Iron-rich foods, screen time limits, dental, poison-proofing, water safety |
| Preschool | Discipline, school readiness, helmet, bullying |
| School-age | Healthy eating, exercise, screen time, oral health, safety |

**Primitive reflexes (when they should disappear):**

| Reflex | Age of disappearance |
|--------|---------------------|
| Moro | 4-6 mo |
| Palmar grasp | 4-6 mo |
| Rooting | 4 mo |
| Asymmetric tonic neck (ATNR) | 6 mo |
| Babinski | 12-24 mo |

Persistence beyond expected → consider CNS injury (cerebral palsy).

**Growth screening:**

| Age | Tracks |
|-----|--------|
| 0-2 yr | WHO chart (international); HC, weight, length |
| 2+ yr | CDC chart; BMI from age 2 |

**Common gotchas:**
- **CDC 2022 milestones** lowered to "75%" benchmarks — anything missed past listed age = referral, not "wait and see"
- **First MMR after 12 mo** — earlier doses don't count as routine (give measles vaccine at 6 mo for travel/outbreak but redose)
- **Rotavirus** — must start by 14 wk 6 d, complete by 8 mo
- **HPV** — 9 yr is the minimum starting age; earlier (9-14) gets 2-dose schedule
- **Lead screening** at 12 + 24 mo for Medicaid-eligible / high-risk; capillary screen → confirm with venous
- **Anemia screening** at 12 mo (hemoglobin)
- **Vision** screened at 3-4 yr; **hearing** at every visit, formal test at school age
- **Newborn hearing screen** must follow up if abnormal

**Rule of thumb:** Track milestones on the CDC 2022 75th-percentile chart; anything missed → refer. Vaccinate per schedule; live vaccines avoid in immunocompromised. Use HEEADSSS for adolescents. Anticipatory guidance ≠ optional.
