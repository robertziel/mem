### Cardiac Cycle (Pressure-Volume Loop)

**Four phases (LV-centric):**

| Phase | Volume change | Valves | Pressure change |
|-------|---------------|--------|-----------------|
| 1. Isovolumetric contraction | Constant (~120 mL) | All closed | LV pressure rises rapidly |
| 2. Ejection | Volume falls (120→50 mL) | Aortic open | LV pressure peaks then falls |
| 3. Isovolumetric relaxation | Constant (~50 mL) | All closed | LV pressure falls rapidly |
| 4. Filling | Volume rises (50→120 mL) | Mitral open | LV pressure low |

**Heart sounds:**

| Sound | Cause | Timing |
|-------|-------|--------|
| S1 | Mitral + tricuspid closure | Start of systole |
| S2 | Aortic + pulmonic closure | Start of diastole |
| S3 | Rapid ventricular filling | Early diastole — HF, normal in young |
| S4 | Atrial kick into stiff ventricle | Late diastole — HFpEF, HCM, HTN |

**Pressure-volume loop:**
```
        ↑ Pressure
        │   ____
        │  /    \   <- ejection (aortic open)
        │ |      |
   AV opens →   ← AV closes (end-systole)
        │ |      |
        │  \____/   <- filling (mitral open)
   MV closes →    ← MV opens
        │
        └────────→ Volume
        ESV (~50)  EDV (~120)
```

**Key points on the loop:**
- **EDV** (end-diastolic volume): preload, ~120 mL
- **ESV** (end-systolic volume): ~50 mL
- **Stroke volume** = EDV − ESV ≈ 70 mL
- **Ejection fraction** = SV / EDV ≈ 55-65%

**Determinants of stroke volume:**

| Factor | Increases SV | Decreases SV |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Preload | ↑ EDV (volume, venous return) | Hemorrhage, vasodilation |
| Afterload | ↓ resistance (vasodilator) | ↑ aortic pressure, AS |
| Contractility | Sympathetic, Ca++, inotropes | Ischemia, β-blocker, acidosis |

**Loop shifts (recognize at a glance):**

| Disease | Loop change |
|---------|-------------|
| ↑ Preload (volume overload, AR, MR) | Wider loop, EDV right |
| ↑ Afterload (HTN, AS) | Taller loop, peak pressure up |
| ↓ Contractility (HFrEF) | Smaller loop, ESV right (residual volume) |
| HFpEF | Narrow loop, steep diastolic line |

**Common gotchas:**
- Frank-Starling: ↑ preload → ↑ contractile force (length-tension), but only up to a point — overstretched fails
- Afterload kills the failing heart faster than preload
- S3 is normal in kids/athletes; S3 + symptoms in adults = volume overload until proven otherwise

**Rule of thumb:** Read PV loops left-to-right as preload, top-to-bottom as afterload, area = stroke work; HFrEF shifts ESV right, HFpEF shifts EDV left and up.
