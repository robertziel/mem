### Acid-Base Balance

**Henderson-Hasselbalch:** pH = 6.1 + log([HCO3⁻] / 0.03 × PaCO2). Practical: pH tracks HCO3⁻ / CO2.

**Four primary disorders:**

| Disorder | pH | Primary | Compensation |
|----------|----|----|--------------|
| Metabolic acidosis | ↓ | ↓ HCO3⁻ | ↓ CO2 (hyperventilation) |
| Metabolic alkalosis | ↑ | ↑ HCO3⁻ | ↑ CO2 (hypoventilation) |
| Respiratory acidosis | ↓ | ↑ CO2 | ↑ HCO3⁻ (renal, slow) |
| Respiratory alkalosis | ↑ | ↓ CO2 | ↓ HCO3⁻ (renal, slow) |

**Quick interpretation:**
1. pH: acidemic (<7.35) or alkalemic (>7.45)?
2. CO2 vs HCO3⁻: which moves *with* the pH? → primary disorder
3. Compensation appropriate? Use Winters' / expected formulas

**Expected compensation rules:**

| Primary | Expected response |
|---------|-------------------|
| Metabolic acidosis | PaCO2 = 1.5×HCO3⁻ + 8 ± 2 (Winters') |
| Metabolic alkalosis | PaCO2 ↑ 0.7 mmHg per 1 ↑ HCO3⁻ |
| Acute resp acidosis | HCO3⁻ ↑ 1 per 10 ↑ PaCO2 |
| Chronic resp acidosis | HCO3⁻ ↑ 4 per 10 ↑ PaCO2 |
| Acute resp alkalosis | HCO3⁻ ↓ 2 per 10 ↓ PaCO2 |
| Chronic resp alkalosis | HCO3⁻ ↓ 5 per 10 ↓ PaCO2 |

If actual ≠ expected → **mixed disorder**.

**Anion gap:** AG = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO3⁻); normal 8-12. Corrected AG = AG + 2.5×(4 − albumin).

**Anion gap acidosis (MUDPILES):**

| Letter | Cause |
|--------|-------|
| M | Methanol |
| U | Uremia |
| D | DKA |
| P | Paraldehyde / Propylene glycol |
| I | Iron / Isoniazid / Ingestions |
| L | Lactate |
| E | Ethylene glycol |
| S | Salicylates |

**Non-AG (hyperchloremic) acidosis (HARDASS):**
- Hyperalimentation, Addison's, RTA, Diarrhea, Acetazolamide, Spironolactone, Saline

**Metabolic alkalosis chloride-responsive vs resistant:**

| Type | Urine Cl⁻ | Causes |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Cl-responsive | <20 | Vomiting, NG suction, diuretics (late) |
| Cl-resistant | >20 | Hyperaldo (Conn's), Cushing's, Bartter, Gitelman |

**Common gotchas:**
- Salicylates → mixed AG metabolic acidosis + respiratory alkalosis
- DKA may co-exist with vomiting → mixed AG acidosis + metabolic alkalosis
- Always check delta-delta in AG acidosis: ΔAG vs ΔHCO3⁻ reveals hidden non-AG disorder
- Lactate >4 = severe; lactate clearance is the resuscitation marker, not just initial value

**Rule of thumb:** pH → primary → compensation → anion gap → delta-delta. Mixed disorders are common; one number off-pattern means look for a second disorder.
