### Leukemia

**Acute vs chronic, myeloid vs lymphoid:**

| | Lymphoid | Myeloid |
|---|----------|---------|
| Acute | **ALL** | **AML** |
| Chronic | **CLL** | **CML** |

**Side-by-side:**

| Feature | ALL | AML | CML | CLL |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| Population | Children (peak 2-5y); also adults | Older adults (median ~65) | Middle age | Older adults |
| Cell | Lymphoid blasts (B>T) | Myeloid blasts | Myeloid mature spectrum | Mature B cells |
| Marrow blasts | ≥20% | ≥20% | <10% | Bone marrow infiltration |
| Cytogenetics | t(12;21) ETV6-RUNX1 (peds, good); t(9;22) Ph+ (adult, treat with TKI); MLL (KMT2A) infants poor | t(15;17) APML; t(8;21), inv(16) (good); FLT3-ITD, TP53 (bad) | **t(9;22) BCR-ABL Philadelphia** | del(17p) TP53, del(11q) — bad; del(13q) — better |
| Clinical | Pancytopenia, bone pain, hepatosplenomegaly, **CNS, testicular**, mediastinal mass (T-ALL) | Pancytopenia, leukostasis (very high WBC), gum hypertrophy (M5), DIC (APML), Auer rods | Often asymptomatic; ↑↑WBC with all maturation, basophilia, splenomegaly | Lymphocytosis, lymphadenopathy, immune cytopenias (AIHA, ITP), **smudge cells** |
| Treatment | Chemo + intrathecal CNS prophylaxis + maintenance + ± stem cell transplant; Ph+ adds TKI | Chemo (7+3) + consolidation; APML = ATRA + arsenic; targeted (FLT3, IDH inhibitors, venetoclax) | **Imatinib / dasatinib / nilotinib (TKI)** — chronic phase often controllable for years | Watch & wait if asymptomatic; BTK inhibitor (ibrutinib, acalabrutinib), venetoclax, CD20 mAb (rituximab, obinutuzumab) |

**APML (M3) — special:**

| Feature |
|---------|
| **t(15;17)** PML-RARA |
| Severe **DIC** at presentation |
| **All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA) + arsenic trioxide** — start as soon as suspected |
| Differentiation syndrome — fever, dyspnea, edema; treat with **dexamethasone** |

**CML phases:**

| Phase | Marrow blasts |
|-------|---------------|
| Chronic | <10% |
| Accelerated | 10-19% |
| Blast crisis | ≥20% (looks like acute leukemia) |

TKI response monitored by **BCR-ABL transcript (PCR)**.

**CLL — Rai/Binet staging (simplified):**

| Stage | Findings |
|-------|----------|
| 0 / Low (Rai 0; Binet A) | Lymphocytosis only |
| Intermediate (Rai I-II) | Lymphadenopathy or organomegaly |
| High (Rai III-IV; Binet C) | Anemia or thrombocytopenia |

Treat for: symptomatic disease, progressive cytopenia (not autoimmune), bulky / progressive nodes, B symptoms, recurrent infections.

**Acute leukemia presentation triad:**
1. Pancytopenia (anemia, thrombocytopenia → bleeding, neutropenia → infection)
2. Marrow replacement (bone pain, hepatosplenomegaly)
3. Tissue infiltration (mediastinum, CNS, testes, gums)

**Tumor lysis syndrome (TLS):**

| Hallmark | Lab |
|----------|-----|
| Hyperuricemia | High urate |
| Hyperkalemia | High K |
| Hyperphosphatemia | High PO4 |
| Hypocalcemia | Low Ca (binds PO4) |
| AKI | From urate, calcium phosphate |

Risk: high tumor burden, rapidly proliferative (Burkitt, ALL, AML, CML blast crisis), high LDH/uric acid, baseline renal disease.

| Prevention | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Hydration | Aggressive |
| **Allopurinol** | For low-mod risk |
| **Rasburicase** | High risk; converts urate → allantoin (water-soluble); avoid in G6PD deficiency |

**Leukostasis:** WBC >50-100 K → CNS, pulmonary, retinal symptoms; **leukapheresis emergency**, hydroxyurea, induction therapy.

**Differential of lymphocytosis:**

| Cause | Clue |
|-------|------|
| CLL | Smudge cells, mature B cells, persistent |
| Reactive (viral) | Atypical lymphocytes, transient |
| Whooping cough (pertussis) | Children |
| Hairy cell leukemia | "Dry tap", TRAP+, splenomegaly, pancytopenia |
| Mantle cell, marginal zone, follicular | Specific pathology |

**Auer rods** — pathognomonic for AML (especially APML).

**Smudge cells** — classic CLL on smear.

**Common gotchas:**
- **Suspect APML** in any AML with DIC — start ATRA empirically before genetics return
- **Don't delay TKI** in CML accelerated phase
- **Watch for differentiation syndrome** in APML on ATRA — dexamethasone
- CLL with rising lymph node + LDH → **Richter transformation** (to DLBCL)
- ALL needs CNS prophylaxis (intrathecal); chemo doesn't cross BBB well
- Adult ALL with t(9;22) Ph+ → add TKI (imatinib) to chemo

**Rule of thumb:** Acute = blasts ≥20%, sick fast. Chronic = mature spectrum, slow. APML emergency: ATRA + arsenic. CML: BCR-ABL TKI. CLL: smudge cells, watch & wait if low stage; BTK inhibitor / venetoclax if treating. TLS prophylaxis with allopurinol or rasburicase before chemo.
