### Anemia Workup

**MCV-based first split:**

| MCV | Type | Next test |
|-----|------|-----------|
| <80 | Microcytic | Iron studies, then Hb electrophoresis |
| 80-100 | Normocytic | Reticulocyte count |
| >100 | Macrocytic | B12, folate, ± LFTs, TSH |

**Microcytic — iron studies pattern:**

| Disease | Iron | TIBC | Ferritin | TSAT | Other |
|---------|------|------|----------|------|-------|
| **IDA** | ↓ | ↑ | **↓** | ↓ | RDW ↑, target/pencil cells |
| **Anemia of chronic disease** | ↓ | ↓ | normal/↑ | ↓ | Inflammatory state |
| **Thalassemia** | normal | normal | normal | normal | **Target cells**, basophilic stippling, MCV very low for Hgb |
| **Sideroblastic** | ↑ | normal | ↑ | ↑ | Ring sideroblasts (Prussian blue); EtOH, INH, lead, MDS |

Mnemonic: **TICS** for microcytic — Thalassemia, IDA, Chronic disease (ACD), Sideroblastic.

**IDA causes by patient:**

| Group | Cause |
|-------|-------|
| Premenopausal woman | **Menstrual loss** most common; pregnancy |
| Older adult | **GI cancer** until proven otherwise — colonoscopy + EGD |
| Children | Cow's milk excess, dietary |
| Vegetarians | Dietary |
| Celiac | Malabsorption |
| H. pylori, gastric bypass | Achlorhydria, malabsorption |

**Macrocytic — megaloblastic vs non-megaloblastic:**

| | Megaloblastic | Non-megaloblastic |
|---|---------------|-------------------|
| Mechanism | DNA synthesis defect | Membrane / lipid issue |
| Hypersegmented neutrophils | **Yes** | No |
| Causes | B12, folate, drugs (MTX, TMP, hydroxyurea, AZT, phenytoin) | Alcohol, liver disease, hypothyroid, reticulocytosis, MDS |

**B12 vs folate (recap):**

| Feature | B12 | Folate |
|---------|-----|--------|
| Megaloblastic anemia | Yes | Yes |
| Hypersegmented neutrophils | Yes | Yes |
| Methylmalonic acid | ↑ | Normal |
| Homocysteine | ↑ | ↑ |
| Neuro symptoms (SCD) | **Yes** | No |
| Causes | Pernicious, terminal ileum, vegan | Pregnancy, MTX, alcohol, phenytoin |

Always check **B12 first** — folate alone corrects anemia but **lets neuro damage progress**.

**Normocytic — reticulocyte count splits:**

| Retic | Cause |
|-------|-------|
| Low (<2%) | **Hypoproliferative** — early IDA, ACD, CKD (↓EPO), aplastic anemia, MDS, infiltration |
| High (>2%) | **Hemolysis** or **acute blood loss** |

**Hemolytic anemia workup:**

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| ↑ LDH | Cell breakdown |
| ↓ Haptoglobin | Binds free Hb |
| ↑ Indirect bilirubin | Heme breakdown |
| Hemoglobinuria, schistocytes | Intravascular |
| Spherocytes | HS, AIHA (warm) |
| Bite cells, Heinz bodies | G6PD, oxidative |
| Sickle cells | Sickle disease |
| Schistocytes (helmet, fragmented) | TTP, HUS, DIC, mech valve, HELLP |
| Target cells | Thal, liver disease, post-splenectomy |
| Howell-Jolly bodies | Asplenia |

**Coombs test:**

| Direct (DAT) | Pathology |
|--------------|-----------|
| Positive (IgG ± C3) — warm AIHA | Treat: steroids → rituximab → splenectomy; SLE, CLL, drugs (penicillin, methyldopa, cefotetan), idiopathic |
| Positive (C3 only) — cold AIHA | Mycoplasma, EBV, CLL, lymphoma; avoid cold; rituximab |
| **Negative** | Hereditary (G6PD, sickle, HS, PK), microangiopathic, drug-induced |

**Hereditary microangiopathic / structural:**

| Disease | Defect | Clue |
|---------|--------|------|
| Hereditary spherocytosis | Ankyrin / spectrin / band 3 | ↑MCHC, splenomegaly, jaundice; **EMA binding** test; treat splenectomy in severe |
| G6PD deficiency | X-linked | Episodic hemolysis after oxidative stress (sulfa, dapsone, fava beans, infection); **Heinz bodies, bite cells** |
| Sickle cell | β-globin Glu→Val | Vaso-occlusive crises, autosplenectomy → encapsulated infections |
| Thalassemia | α or β globin | Severity by gene number |
| Pyruvate kinase deficiency | AR | Chronic non-spherocytic hemolysis |
| PNH | PIG-A → loss of CD55/CD59 | Intravascular hemolysis, thrombosis (esp. hepatic vein), pancytopenia |

**Pancytopenia differential:**

| Cause |
|-------|
| Aplastic anemia (idiopathic, drugs, viral, radiation) |
| MDS (older, dysplastic) |
| Acute leukemia |
| Marrow infiltration (cancer, fibrosis, infection) |
| Severe B12/folate def |
| PNH |
| Sepsis, hypersplenism |

Bone marrow biopsy resolves most.

**Thalassemia patterns:**

| Type | Genes | Clue |
|------|-------|------|
| α-thal silent (1 deletion) | -α/αα | Asymptomatic |
| α-thal trait (2 deletions) | --/αα or -α/-α | Mild microcytic anemia, normal Hb electrophoresis (cis vs trans matters for genetics) |
| HbH (3 deletions) | --/-α | Moderate-severe; HbH (β4) on electrophoresis |
| Hb Bart (4 deletions) | --/-- | Hydrops fetalis, in utero death |
| β-thal minor (heterozygous) | β/β⁺ or β/β⁰ | Mild; **↑HbA2 (>3.5%)** on electrophoresis |
| β-thal major (homozygous) | β⁰/β⁰ | Cooley anemia, transfusion-dependent, iron overload |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Iron repletion** in IDA: oral every other day actually has **better absorption** than daily (hepcidin response); IV (ferric gluconate, sucrose, carboxymaltose) for malabs / intolerance / CKD
- **Reticulocyte index** (correct for low Hgb) more accurate than raw retic %
- **Smear is a high-yield free test** — always look
- **Cold agglutinins** can cause falsely high MCV/MCH
- **Thalassemia trait** has microcytosis disproportionate to anemia — **Mentzer index** (MCV / RBC count) <13 → thal; >13 → IDA

**Rule of thumb:** MCV → category. Microcytic = TICS. Macrocytic megaloblastic = B12 + folate. Normocytic with high retic = hemolysis. Smear + reticulocyte + iron studies + B12/folate covers most workups.
