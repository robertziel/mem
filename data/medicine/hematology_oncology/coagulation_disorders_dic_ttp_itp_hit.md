### Coagulation Disorders

**Quick orientation by lab pattern:**

| Disorder | PT/INR | aPTT | Platelets | Fibrinogen | D-dimer |
|----------|--------|------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Hemophilia A/B | normal | **↑** | normal | normal | normal |
| von Willebrand disease | normal | normal/↑ | normal | normal | normal |
| Vitamin K deficiency / warfarin | **↑** | mild ↑ | normal | normal | normal |
| Liver disease | ↑ | ↑ | ↓ (hypersplenism) | ↓ | ± ↑ |
| **DIC** | **↑** | **↑** | **↓** | **↓** | **↑↑** |
| Heparin | normal | ↑ | normal (HIT lowers) | normal | normal |
| TTP / HUS | normal | normal | **↓** | normal | normal |
| ITP | normal | normal | ↓ | normal | normal |
| HIT | normal | normal | ↓ (50%) + thrombosis | normal | normal |
| Factor V Leiden | normal | normal | normal | normal | normal (clot tendency) |

**Microangiopathic hemolytic anemia (MAHA) differential:**

| Disease | Features |
|---------|----------|
| **TTP** | Pentad: MAHA + thrombocytopenia + AKI + neuro + fever (often partial); **ADAMTS13 <10%** |
| **HUS** | Children, post-bloody diarrhea (E. coli O157:H7); MAHA + thrombocytopenia + AKI |
| **Atypical HUS** | Complement dysregulation; eculizumab |
| **DIC** | Sepsis, OB emergencies, malignancy, trauma |
| **HELLP** | Pregnancy: Hemolysis, ↑Liver, Low Platelets |
| **Mechanical valve** | Hemolysis from prosthesis |
| **Malignant HTN** | Endothelial damage |

All have schistocytes. ADAMTS13 distinguishes TTP. Diarrhea + child → HUS.

**TTP management:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Suspect → **plasma exchange (PLEX) urgently** before ADAMTS13 returns |
| 2 | Steroids |
| 3 | Caplacizumab (anti-vWF) |
| 4 | Rituximab for refractory / immune-mediated |
| **Avoid platelet transfusion** unless severe bleeding (can worsen thrombosis) |

**ITP:**

| Feature |
|---------|
| Isolated thrombocytopenia, otherwise well |
| Diagnosis of exclusion (no schistocytes, normal coags, no infection) |
| Treatment if Plt <30 or bleeding: steroids ± IVIG, anti-D (Rh+) |
| Refractory: rituximab, TPO-RA (eltrombopag, romiplostim), splenectomy |

**HIT:**

| 4 T's score | Points |
|-------------|--------|
| **T**hrombocytopenia (>50% drop or nadir 20-100) | 0-2 |
| **T**iming (5-10 days, or <1 day with prior heparin) | 0-2 |
| **T**hrombosis (new) | 0-2 |
| Other causes (oT)her | 0-2 |

Score ≥4 → test (anti-PF4 antibody, then SRA confirmation).

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Stop ALL heparin (incl. flushes)** |
| 2 | **Start argatroban or fondaparinux or DOAC** (NOT warfarin alone — limb gangrene) |
| 3 | Once platelets ≥150 and stable, transition to warfarin with overlap or continue DOAC |
| 4 | Anticoagulate at least 4 weeks; longer if thrombosis |

Avoid platelet transfusion (worsens thrombosis).

**von Willebrand disease:**

| Type | Defect | Treatment |
|------|--------|-----------|
| 1 (most common, mild) | Quantitative ↓ vWF | DDAVP + tranexamic acid; vWF concentrate if severe |
| 2 (qualitative) | Several subtypes | vWF concentrate; DDAVP can worsen 2B |
| 3 (severe) | Almost no vWF | vWF + factor VIII concentrate |

vWD: mucocutaneous bleeding (epistaxis, menorrhagia, gum), normal platelet count, ↑ aPTT (vWF stabilizes factor VIII).

**Hemophilia A vs B:**

| | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Factor | VIII | IX |
| Inheritance | X-linked recessive | X-linked recessive |
| Treatment | Factor VIII concentrate, desmopressin (mild), emicizumab | Factor IX concentrate |
| Lab | ↑aPTT, normal PT, normal Plts | Same |

**DIC clue:** Underlying severe condition (sepsis, malignancy [APML], OB [amniotic fluid embolism, abruption], trauma, transfusion, snake bite) + bleeding + thrombosis paradoxically.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Treat underlying cause |
| 2 | Replace if bleeding: FFP (factors), cryoprecipitate (fibrinogen), platelets |
| 3 | APML — give all-trans retinoic acid early |

**Inherited thrombophilias:**

| Disorder | Mechanism |
|----------|-----------|
| **Factor V Leiden** | Factor V resistant to APC inactivation (most common inherited thrombophilia) |
| Prothrombin G20210A | ↑ prothrombin level |
| Protein C / S deficiency | Lose anticoagulant; warfarin-induced skin necrosis (start with heparin overlap) |
| Antithrombin deficiency | Heparin "resistance" (heparin works through AT) |
| Antiphospholipid syndrome | Acquired; recurrent VTE / stroke / pregnancy loss; **lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin, anti-β2-glycoprotein I**; **warfarin only** (DOACs inferior, esp. triple-positive) |

**When to test thrombophilia (not routinely):**

| Indication |
|-----------|
| Unprovoked VTE under 50 |
| Recurrent VTE |
| Family history strong |
| VTE in unusual sites (cerebral, splanchnic) |
| Pregnancy loss / APS suspicion |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Warfarin-induced skin necrosis** — early, in protein C/S deficient patients (transient hypercoagulable state); always **bridge with heparin** when starting warfarin
- **HIT timing**: 5-10 days; can occur <1 day with prior exposure ("rapid onset") or >10 days ("delayed-onset")
- **APS triple-positive (LA + aCL + anti-β2GPI)** has highest thrombosis risk — warfarin only
- **D-dimer is sensitive but not specific** — only useful in low pre-test probability or trending in DIC
- Don't transfuse platelets in TTP, HIT, atypical HUS unless life-threatening bleed

**Rule of thumb:** Lab pattern → diagnosis. TTP/HUS/DIC/HELLP — schistocytes triage. HIT: stop heparin, switch to argatroban/DOAC, never warfarin alone. APS / mechanical valve / mod-severe MS = warfarin only.
