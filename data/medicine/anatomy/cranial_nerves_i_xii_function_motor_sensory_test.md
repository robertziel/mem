### Cranial Nerves I-XII

**Mnemonic (function):** Some Say Marry Money But My Brother Says Big Brains Matter More — S=Sensory, M=Motor, B=Both.

| # | Name | Type | Function | Bedside test |
|---|------|------|----------|--------------|
| I | Olfactory | S | Smell | Familiar smell each nostril |
| II | Optic | S | Vision | Acuity, fields, fundoscopy |
| III | Oculomotor | M | EOM (most), pupil constrict, lid elevation | Pupil reflex, EOMs, ptosis |
| IV | Trochlear | M | Superior oblique (eye down/in) | Look down + medial |
| V | Trigeminal | B | Face sensation, mastication | Light touch V1/V2/V3, clench, corneal |
| VI | Abducens | M | Lateral rectus | Abduction |
| VII | Facial | B | Facial expression, taste ant 2/3, lacrimation | Smile, raise brows, close eyes |
| VIII | Vestibulocochlear | S | Hearing, balance | Whisper, Weber/Rinne, head impulse |
| IX | Glossopharyngeal | B | Pharynx sense, taste post 1/3 | Gag (afferent), palate elevation |
| X | Vagus | B | Pharynx/larynx motor, PNS visceral | Uvula, hoarseness, gag (efferent) |
| XI | Accessory | M | SCM, trapezius | Shrug, head turn vs resistance |
| XII | Hypoglossal | M | Tongue movement | Stick tongue out |

**Localizing common lesions:**

| Finding | Lesion |
|---------|--------|
| "Down and out" eye, ptosis, dilated pupil | CN III complete |
| Vertical diplopia worsens looking down/in | CN IV |
| Failed abduction, horizontal diplopia | CN VI |
| Hemifacial weakness sparing forehead | UMN VII (cortical) |
| Hemifacial weakness incl. forehead | LMN VII (Bell, peripheral) |
| Tongue deviates **toward** weak side | CN XII LMN |
| Uvula deviates **away** from weak side | CN X |

**Brainstem level:**
- Midbrain: III, IV
- Pons: V, VI, VII, VIII
- Medulla: IX, X, XI, XII

**Pupillary reflex:** Afferent CN II → midbrain (Edinger-Westphal) → Efferent CN III. Direct + consensual.

**Gotchas:**
- CN III palsy + pupil involvement → compression (PCom aneurysm) until proven otherwise; pupil-sparing → microvascular (DM)
- "Tongue toward weakness" = LMN; UMN deviates away
- Forehead-sparing = central VII (bilateral cortical input)

**Rule of thumb:** Test by number, decide UMN vs LMN, then localize to brainstem level — pupil involvement in CN III is the urgent finding.
