### Brachial Plexus

**Mnemonic structure:** Real Texans Drink Cold Beer — Roots → Trunks → Divisions → Cords → Branches.

| Level | Components |
|-------|------------|
| **Roots** | C5, C6, C7, C8, T1 (ventral rami) |
| **Trunks** | Upper (C5-C6), Middle (C7), Lower (C8-T1) |
| **Divisions** | Each trunk → anterior + posterior |
| **Cords** | Lateral, Posterior, Medial (named by relation to axillary artery) |
| **Branches** | Terminal nerves to upper limb |

**Five terminal branches:**

| Nerve | Cord origin | Roots | Major motor | Sensory |
|-------|-------------|-------|-------------|---------|
| Musculocutaneous | Lateral | C5-C7 | Biceps, brachialis (elbow flexion) | Lateral forearm |
| Axillary | Posterior | C5-C6 | Deltoid (abduction), teres minor | Lateral shoulder ("regimental badge") |
| Radial | Posterior | C5-T1 | Triceps + all wrist/finger extensors | Dorsum hand (1st webspace) |
| Median | Lateral + Medial | C5-T1 | Forearm flexors (most), thenar | Palmar lateral 3.5 digits |
| Ulnar | Medial | C8-T1 | Intrinsic hand (most), FCU | Palmar medial 1.5 digits |

**Common injuries:**

| Lesion | Site | Findings |
|--------|------|----------|
| Erb's palsy | Upper trunk (C5-C6) | "Waiter's tip" — adducted, internally rotated, pronated |
| Klumpke's palsy | Lower trunk (C8-T1) | Claw hand, intrinsic muscle weakness ± Horner's |
| Surgical neck humerus fx | Axillary | Deltoid wasting, lateral shoulder numbness |
| Midshaft humerus fx | Radial | Wrist drop, lost finger extension |
| Supracondylar humerus fx | Median (AIN) | "OK sign" lost (FPL + FDP index) |
| Medial epicondyle / Guyon's | Ulnar | Claw hand (4th-5th), Froment's sign |
| Carpal tunnel | Median (compression) | Thenar wasting, palmar 3.5 numb |

**Quick localization clues:**
- Wrist drop → radial
- Loss of thumb opposition → median (recurrent thenar branch)
- Hypothenar wasting → ulnar
- Deltoid + lateral shoulder sensation → axillary
- Inability to flex elbow + lateral forearm numbness → musculocutaneous

**Common gotchas:**
- Radial nerve does **everything extensor** above the wrist (and BR for elbow)
- Median + ulnar split the hand intrinsics: median = LOAF (Lumbricals 1-2, Opponens, APB, FPB); ulnar = the rest
- "Saturday night palsy" (radial compression in spiral groove) = wrist drop with **preserved triceps**

**Rule of thumb:** RTDCB structure; localize by motor exam (deltoid → axillary, wrist extension → radial, thumb opposition → median, intrinsics → ulnar) before reaching for imaging.
