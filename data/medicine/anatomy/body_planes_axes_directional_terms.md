### Anatomical Planes, Axes, Directional Terms

**Planes (cut the body):**

| Plane | Divides into | Common imaging |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Sagittal | Left / right (midsagittal = exact midline) | MRI brain side view |
| Coronal (frontal) | Anterior / posterior | MRI brain front view |
| Transverse (axial, horizontal) | Superior / inferior | CT slice |
| Oblique | Any non-orthogonal | Special views |

**Directional pairs:**

| Term | Opposite | Meaning |
|------|----------|---------|
| Superior (cranial) | Inferior (caudal) | Toward head / toward feet |
| Anterior (ventral) | Posterior (dorsal) | Toward front / toward back |
| Medial | Lateral | Toward midline / away |
| Proximal | Distal | Toward trunk / away (used for limbs) |
| Superficial | Deep | Toward surface / away |
| Ipsilateral | Contralateral | Same side / opposite side |
| Palmar (volar) | Dorsal | Palm of hand / back of hand |
| Plantar | Dorsal | Sole of foot / top of foot |

**Movement terms:**

| Movement | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Flexion / Extension | Decrease / increase joint angle |
| Abduction / Adduction | Away from / toward midline |
| Rotation (internal/external) | Turning around long axis |
| Pronation / Supination | Forearm: palm down / palm up |
| Inversion / Eversion | Sole faces medial / lateral |
| Dorsiflexion / Plantarflexion | Foot up toward shin / down |
| Circumduction | Cone-shaped combined motion |

**Position terms:**
- **Anatomical position** — standing, palms forward, feet together. All directional terms reference this.
- **Supine** — face up
- **Prone** — face down
- **Lateral decubitus** — lying on side (right or left)

**Common gotchas:**
- "Volar" vs "palmar" — synonymous on the hand
- "Dorsal" means back of trunk, but back of *hand* and *top* of foot
- Proximal/distal apply to limbs (and tubular structures), not trunk
- Always ipsilateral/contralateral relative to a defined reference, not arbitrary

**Rule of thumb:** Always describe relative to anatomical position; for limbs use proximal/distal; for trunk use superior/inferior + anterior/posterior.
