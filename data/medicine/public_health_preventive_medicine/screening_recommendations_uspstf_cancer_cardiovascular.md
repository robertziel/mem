### Adult Preventive Screening — USPSTF Highlights

**USPSTF grades:**

| Grade | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| **A** | Strong recommend |
| **B** | Recommend |
| **C** | Selective offer |
| **D** | Recommend against |
| **I** | Insufficient evidence |

Insurance covers grade A/B without copay (ACA).

**Cancer screening — high-yield (US adults, average risk):**

| Cancer | Test | Age range | Frequency |
|--------|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Colorectal** | Colonoscopy / FIT / FIT-DNA / sigmoidoscopy / CT colonography | **45-75** (lowered from 50 in 2021) | Colonoscopy q10y, FIT annually, FIT-DNA q3y, sig q5y, CTC q5y; selective 76-85 |
| **Breast** | Mammography | **40-74** (USPSTF 2024 — lowered from 50) | Biennial |
| **Cervical** | Cytology / HPV / co-test | 21-65 | Cytology q3y (21-29); HPV q5y or co-test q5y (30-65) |
| **Lung** | LDCT | **50-80** with 20 pack-yr history, current smoker or quit ≤15 yr | Annual |
| **Prostate (PSA)** | C grade 55-69 (shared decision) | — | Discuss |
| **Skin** | I grade (insufficient — but high-risk patients screen) | — | — |
| **Pancreatic, ovarian, testicular** | D grade in average risk | — | — |
| **Hepatocellular carcinoma** | US ± AFP for cirrhosis / chronic HBV | At-risk | Q6 mo |

**Cardiovascular screening:**

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| **Hypertension** | Adults ≥18 — measure at every visit; confirm with home / ABPM |
| **Lipid (cholesterol)** | Adults 40-75 (statin assessment, **PCE risk score**); selective for younger with risk factors |
| **AAA** | Men 65-75 who **ever smoked** — 1× US |
| **Aspirin for primary prevention** | Generally **not recommended** for primary prevention <40 or ≥60; **selectively 40-59** with high CV risk + low bleeding risk + shared decision |
| **Statin for primary prevention** | Adults 40-75 with ≥1 CV risk factor + 10-yr CVD risk ≥10% (B); 7.5-10% (C); calculate with PCE / pooled cohort |
| **Diabetes** | Adults 35-70 with BMI ≥25; A1c or fasting glucose |
| **Smoking** | Ask + advise + assist + arrange every visit |
| **Obesity** | BMI; refer to behavioral if ≥30 |
| **Aspirin for preeclampsia prevention** | High-risk pregnancy 12-28 wk |

**Infectious disease screening:**

| Test | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| HIV | Universal once (15-65), pregnancy, more often if at risk |
| Hepatitis B | Universal once for adults; pregnancy each |
| Hepatitis C | Universal once for adults; pregnancy each |
| Latent TB | Targeted high-risk (HIV, immunosuppression, high-prev country, contacts) |
| Syphilis | High-risk (MSM, IVDU, prior STI, high prevalence) and pregnancy |
| Chlamydia / gonorrhea | Sexually active women ≤24, older women + men with risk |
| HPV | Co-test 30-65 |

| Topic | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| Depression / anxiety | All adolescents and adults |
| Suicide risk | Adolescents and adults — selectively |
| Intimate partner violence | Reproductive-age women |
| Unhealthy alcohol | Adults — screen + brief counseling |
| Drug use | Adults |
| Falls (older) | Multifactorial |
| Osteoporosis | **Women ≥65, men 70+ or postmenopausal women <65 with risk factors** (FRAX) |

| Dental | Annual |

**Vaccinations (adult, simplified — see CDC):**

| Vaccine | Adult schedule |
|---------|----------------|
| Influenza | Annually all ≥6 mo |
| **Tdap / Td** | Tdap once + Td/Tdap every 10y; Tdap every pregnancy (27-36 wk) |
| **Pneumococcal** | PCV15/20/21 ± PPSV23 for ≥65 or younger with conditions |
| **Zoster (Shingrix)** | ≥50, immunocompromised ≥18; 2 doses |
| **HPV** | Routine 11-12 (catch-up to 26; selective 27-45) |
| Hepatitis A, B | Universal HepB for adults; HepA for high-risk |
| Meningococcal | College freshmen, military, asplenia, complement def |
| **COVID-19** | Per current guidance |
| **RSV** | ≥75 (some ≥60 with risk); pregnant 32-36 wk |
| MMR, varicella | Live — confirm immunity if not previously immune |
| Travel | Yellow fever, typhoid, etc. — destination-specific |

**ASCVD risk calculator (Pooled Cohort Equation):**
- Age, sex, race
- Total cholesterol, HDL
- Systolic BP + treatment status
- DM, smoking
- Output: 10-year ASCVD risk

**Statin intensity:**

| Patient | Intensity |
|---------|-----------|
| Clinical ASCVD | **High** (atorvastatin 40-80, rosuvastatin 20-40) |
| LDL ≥190 | High |
| DM age 40-75 + LDL 70-189 | Moderate-high |
| Primary prevention age 40-75 + 10-yr risk ≥7.5-10% | Moderate-high (shared decision) |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Colorectal screening starts at 45** now — many physicians still default to 50
- **Mammography starts at 40** per USPSTF 2024 (was 50)
- **AAA screening only for ever-smokers** — 1× ultrasound 65-75
- **Aspirin** for primary prevention has been pulled back — generally avoid; cardio-renal benefit lost to bleeding in older
- **PSA** is shared decision — controversial; overdiagnosis risk
- **Lung cancer LDCT** starts at 50 with 20 pack-yr — many missed because eligibility unknown
- **Hepatitis C universal screening** once for adults — high-yield in undiagnosed
- Always individualize: family history, prior findings, life expectancy

**Rule of thumb:** USPSTF A/B = recommended + covered. CRC at 45, mammography at 40, lung LDCT 50-80 if 20 pack-yr, AAA 65-75 male smokers. Statins by ASCVD risk score. Universal HCV/HIV/HBV once. Vaccinations annually + age/condition-driven.
