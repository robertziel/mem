### Hypersensitivity Reactions

**ACID mnemonic:** Anaphylactic, Cytotoxic, Immune complex, Delayed.

| Type | Mechanism | Mediator | Onset | Examples |
|------|-----------|----------|-------|----------|
| I (immediate) | IgE on mast cells → degranulation | Histamine, tryptase, leukotrienes | Minutes | Anaphylaxis, allergic asthma, food/drug allergy, atopy |
| II (cytotoxic) | IgG/IgM vs cell-surface antigen → complement, ADCC, opsonization | Complement, NK, macrophages | Hours-days | AIHA, ITP, Goodpasture, rheumatic fever, pemphigus, Graves, MG, transfusion reaction |
| III (immune complex) | Antigen-antibody complexes deposit in tissue → complement → neutrophils | C3a/C5a, neutrophil enzymes | 1-3 weeks | SLE, PSGN, serum sickness, Arthus, polyarteritis nodosa, hypersensitivity pneumonitis |
| IV (delayed) | T-cell mediated (Th1, CTL, macrophages) | IFN-γ, perforin/granzyme | 48-72h | TB skin test, contact dermatitis, transplant rejection, MS, T1DM, Crohn |

**Type II: organ-specific examples:**

| Disease | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Goodpasture | α3 chain of type IV collagen (lung + kidney BM) |
| Pemphigus vulgaris | Desmoglein 3 |
| Bullous pemphigoid | BP180/230 (hemidesmosome) |
| Graves | TSH receptor (stimulating) |
| Myasthenia gravis | ACh receptor (blocking) |
| Lambert-Eaton | Voltage-gated Ca channel |
| AIHA | RBC surface |
| ITP | Platelet GpIIb/IIIa |
| Hyperacute transplant rejection | Preformed antibody to graft |

**Type III: classic examples:**

| Disease | Antigen | Site |
|---------|---------|------|
| SLE | dsDNA, Smith | Skin, kidney, joints |
| PSGN | Streptococcal antigens | Glomerulus |
| Polyarteritis nodosa | HBsAg (some) | Medium vessel |
| Arthus reaction | Local antigen (intradermal) | Local |
| Serum sickness | Foreign protein (antitoxin, drug) | Systemic |
| Hypersensitivity pneumonitis | Inhaled antigen (mold, bird) | Alveoli |

**Type IV — three subpatterns:**

| Pattern | Mediator | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Contact | CD4 Th1 + macrophages | Poison ivy, nickel |
| Tuberculin | CD4 + macrophages | PPD, sarcoid granuloma |
| Cytotoxic | CD8 CTL | Allograft rejection, T1DM β-cell destruction |
| Granulomatous | Persistent antigen + Th1 | TB, sarcoid, Crohn |

**Quick recognition by timing:**

| Onset after exposure | Type |
|----------------------|------|
| Minutes (urticaria, anaphylaxis) | I |
| Hours (transfusion, AIHA flare) | II |
| 1-3 weeks (serum sickness) | III |
| 48-72h (PPD, contact derm) | IV |

**Anaphylaxis essentials:**
- IgE-mediated, biphasic possible (4-12h after recovery)
- Treat: **IM epinephrine 0.3-0.5mg** (lateral thigh), then antihistamine, steroid, fluids, β-agonist
- IgE is **not** measured during acute episode — tryptase peaks at 60-90 min

**Tests:**

| Test | Mechanism | Use |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Skin prick | IgE-mediated | Type I diagnosis |
| RAST / specific IgE | Serum IgE | Type I when skin testing risky |
| Direct Coombs | Antibody on RBC | Type II (AIHA) |
| Indirect Coombs | Antibody in serum | Crossmatch, transfusion |
| Patch test | T-cell | Type IV contact derm |
| PPD / IGRA | T-cell | Type IV (TB exposure) |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Hyperacute transplant rejection** = preformed antibody → minutes (Type II); **acute** rejection = days-weeks (Type IV cellular)
- Pemphigus = **deeper** (suprabasal, "hot brick on a wall"), pemphigoid = **subepidermal** (tense bullae, less sick patient)
- Type III "serum sickness-like" reactions can occur with drugs (β-lactams, sulfa) at 1-2 weeks
- Penicillin-induced AIHA is **Type II** (drug acts as hapten on RBC)

**Rule of thumb:** Time of onset narrows it: minutes (I), hours (II), 1-3 weeks (III), 2-3 days (IV). Tissue-specific antibody = II. Circulating complexes deposit = III. T-cell granuloma or contact derm = IV.
