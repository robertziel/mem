### Immunodeficiencies

**Recognize by **infection pattern** first:**

| Defect | Recurrent infections with | Examples |
|--------|---------------------------|----------|
| B-cell / antibody | Encapsulated bacteria, Giardia, enterovirus | XLA, CVID, IgA def, hyper-IgM |
| T-cell | Viruses, fungi, intracellular bacteria, opportunistic | DiGeorge, SCID, Hyper-IgM |
| Combined (B+T) | Everything | SCID |
| Phagocyte | Catalase+ bacteria (Staph, Aspergillus, Serratia) | CGD, Chediak-Higashi, LAD |
| Complement | Encapsulated (early); Neisseria (terminal) | C2 most common; C5-9 → Neisseria |

**B-cell defects:**

| Disease | Defect | Onset | Clue |
|---------|--------|-------|------|
| **Bruton XLA** | BTK gene (X-linked) | After 6 mo (maternal IgG wanes) | **No B cells**, no germinal centers, no tonsils; enteroviral encephalitis |
| **CVID** | Variable B-cell maturation | Adolescence/adulthood | ↓ Igs, autoimmune, lymphoma, sino-pulm infections |
| **Selective IgA def** | Most common (1:600) | Often asymptomatic | Anaphylaxis to IgA-containing blood, sino-pulm + GI |
| **Hyper-IgM** | CD40L (X-linked) → no class switch | Infancy | High IgM, low everything else; PJP, Crypto |

**T-cell defects:**

| Disease | Defect | Clue |
|---------|--------|------|
| **DiGeorge (22q11)** | Thymic aplasia, neural crest | "CATCH-22" — Cardiac, Abnormal facies, Thymic hypoplasia, Cleft palate, Hypocalcemia (tetany!) |
| **IL-12 receptor def** | ↓ Th1 → ↓ IFN-γ | Mycobacteria, fungi |
| **Job syndrome (AD-HIES)** | STAT3 → ↓ Th17 | High IgE, eczema, "cold" abscesses, retained baby teeth, coarse facies |
| **Chronic mucocutaneous candidiasis** | T-cell against Candida | Skin/mucosa Candida |

**Combined (severe):**

| Disease | Defect | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| **SCID** | ADA, IL-2Rγ, RAG, Jak3 | "Bubble baby"; ↓ T, ± B; CXR: **absent thymus**; treat with BMT, avoid live vax |
| **Wiskott-Aldrich** | WAS gene (X-linked) | **WATER**: Wiskott + Thrombocytopenia + Eczema + Recurrent infections |
| **Ataxia-telangiectasia** | ATM gene (DNA repair) | Cerebellar ataxia, telangiectasias, IgA def, ↑lymphoma, sensitivity to radiation |
| **Hyper-IgE (Job)** | STAT3 | See above |

**Phagocyte defects:**

| Disease | Defect | Clue |
|---------|--------|------|
| **CGD** | NADPH oxidase | Catalase+ infections (Staph, Burkholderia, Serratia, Nocardia, Aspergillus); abnormal **DHR / NBT test** |
| **Chediak-Higashi** | LYST (lysosome trafficking) | Albinism, neuropathy, recurrent pyogenic, **giant granules** in neutrophils |
| **LAD** (type 1) | β2 integrin (CD18) | **Delayed umbilical separation**, no pus despite leukocytosis, recurrent skin/mucosal |
| **Myeloperoxidase def** | MPO | Often asymptomatic; Candida infections |

**Complement:**

| Defect | Clue |
|--------|------|
| C1 esterase inhibitor | Hereditary angioedema (no urticaria, ACEi worsens) |
| C2/C3 | Encapsulated bacterial infections, glomerulonephritis |
| C5-C9 (MAC) | **Recurrent Neisseria** (gono, meningitidis) |
| DAF/CD59 | PNH (intravascular hemolysis) |

**Secondary immunodeficiency** (more common than primary):
- HIV (CD4 lymphopenia)
- Chemo, transplant immunosuppression
- Malnutrition, diabetes, alcohol
- Splenectomy / asplenia → encapsulated bacteria
- Nephrotic syndrome (Ig loss in urine)

**HIV opportunistic infections by CD4:**

| CD4 | At risk for |
|-----|-------------|
| <500 | Oral candidiasis, oral hairy leukoplakia (EBV), Kaposi (HHV-8), zoster |
| <200 | **PJP, toxo, cryptococcal meningitis** |
| <100 | CMV (retinitis, colitis), HSV |
| <50 | **MAC**, CMV, primary CNS lymphoma (EBV) |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Live vaccines contraindicated** in SCID, severe T-cell defects (and AIDS with CD4 <200)
- **Asplenic** → vaccinate vs encapsulated: Strep pneumo, H. flu B, Neisseria
- **DiGeorge** is a 22q11.2 deletion — check FISH; calcium drop within hours
- **CGD test**: DHR (newer) or NBT — both measure oxidative burst
- **HAE** is **not** allergic — bradykinin, not histamine; antihistamines/steroids fail; treat with C1-INH, ecallantide, icatibant

**Rule of thumb:** Encapsulated → B-cell or complement; viruses/fungi → T-cell; catalase+ → phagocyte; Neisseria → terminal complement; all of the above → SCID. Treat ID first, vaccinate (often Ig replacement), avoid live vaccines if T-cell out.
