### Viral Hepatitis A-E

**Compare:**

| Virus | Genome | Transmission | Acute / chronic | Vaccine | Treatment |
|-------|--------|--------------|-----------------|---------|-----------|
| HAV | (+)ssRNA, picorna | Fecal-oral | Acute only | Yes | Supportive |
| HBV | partial dsDNA, hepadna | Blood, sex, vertical | Both — chronic ~5% adult, ~90% perinatal | Yes | Tenofovir, entecavir |
| HCV | (+)ssRNA, flavi | Blood (IVDU > sex) | Chronic ~75-85% | **No** | DAAs (~95% cure) |
| HDV | (−)ssRNA, deltavirus | Blood (with HBV) | Co or super | (HBV vaccine) | Pegylated IFN, bulevirtide |
| HEV | (+)ssRNA, hepe | Fecal-oral, undercooked pork | Acute (chronic in immunocompromised); **fatal in pregnancy** | (in some countries) | Supportive |

**HBV serology — the high-yield table:**

| HBsAg | Anti-HBs | IgM anti-HBc | IgG anti-HBc | HBeAg | Interpretation |
|-------|----------|--------------|--------------|-------|----------------|
| − | − | − | − | − | Susceptible |
| − | + | − | − | − | Vaccinated |
| − | + | − | + | − | Resolved infection |
| **+** | − | **+** | − | + | **Acute** infection |
| + | − | − | + | + | Chronic, replicative ("e+") |
| + | − | − | + | − | Chronic, low replication ("e−") |
| − | − | + | + | − | "Window phase" — only anti-HBc IgM positive |

**HBV phase summary (chronic):**

| Phase | HBeAg | HBV DNA | ALT | Action |
|-------|-------|---------|-----|--------|
| Immune tolerant | + | High | Normal | Monitor |
| Immune active (e+) | + | High | High | **Treat** |
| Inactive carrier (e−) | − | Low | Normal | Monitor |
| Reactivation (e−) | − | High | High | **Treat** |

**HBV treatment indications (any one):**
- HBeAg+ active phase with elevated ALT and high DNA
- HBeAg− with elevated DNA and ALT or fibrosis
- Cirrhosis (any DNA)
- Pregnancy with high VL (third trimester) — to prevent vertical
- Immunosuppression / chemo / biologic — prophylactic

**HBV reactivation prophylaxis** (anti-HBc+ before chemo, rituximab, anti-TNF, transplant) — **prophylactic entecavir or tenofovir**.

**HCV — DAA era essentials:**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Screening | Universal once for adults; pregnant women each pregnancy |
| Confirmation | Anti-HCV positive → HCV RNA |
| Treatment | Pan-genotypic DAAs (sofosbuvir/velpatasvir, glecaprevir/pibrentasvir) — 8-12 wk; SVR12 ~95% |
| Pre-treatment | HBV serology (HBV reactivation possible during DAA), eGFR, prior treatment, cirrhosis status |
| Post-cure surveillance | Cirrhotics still need HCC surveillance (HCC risk persists) |

**Acute hepatitis presentation patterns:**

| Pattern | Likely |
|---------|--------|
| Sudden severe with hepatic failure | HAV, HBV (especially with HDV), drug-induced (acetaminophen) |
| Insidious + chronic course | HBV, HCV, autoimmune, NASH, hemochromatosis |
| Cholestatic | PBC, PSC, drug, obstruction |

**Liver enzyme patterns (orientation):**

| Pattern | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| Hepatocellular | ALT > AST, both ↑↑↑ |
| Cholestatic | Alk phos ↑↑, GGT ↑, mild ALT/AST ↑ |
| Mixed | Both |

**ALT > 1000:** acetaminophen, ischemia, viral hepatitis (acute), autoimmune flare. Drug + ischemia rise/fall faster than viral.

**Common gotchas:**
- **HBV core antibody alone (window phase)** — true infection; check HBV DNA
- **HBV vaccine = anti-HBs only**; recovered = anti-HBs **+ anti-HBc**
- **HEV in pregnancy** — 20-25% mortality (mechanism unclear)
- **Don't miss HDV** in HBV with severe / sudden worsening
- HCC risk persists after **HCV cure** if cirrhotic — keep surveillance
- Tenofovir (TDF or TAF): TAF has less renal/bone toxicity; preferred long-term

**Rule of thumb:** HAV/HEV = fecal-oral, acute only. HBV/HCV = blood/sex, chronic possible. HDV needs HBV. HEV is dangerous in pregnancy. HCV is curable. HBV serology = HBsAg status, then anti-HBc (IgM acute, IgG resolved/chronic), then anti-HBs (immune).
