### Stroke — Ischemic vs Hemorrhagic

**Initial split:**

| Type | Frequency | Imaging |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Ischemic | ~85% | CT often **normal early**; CTA / MRI for definitive |
| Hemorrhagic | ~15% | CT shows **hyperdensity** immediately |

Always **CT head first** — distinguishes hemorrhage from ischemic before reperfusion therapy.

**Ischemic stroke subtypes (TOAST):**

| Subtype | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| Large-artery atherosclerosis | Carotid, aortic arch, intracranial |
| Cardioembolic | AF, LV thrombus, mechanical valve, endocarditis, PFO with paradoxical |
| Small-vessel (lacunar) | HTN, DM; basal ganglia, thalamus, pons |
| Cryptogenic | No cause found despite workup |
| Other | Dissection, vasculitis, hypercoagulable, illicit drugs |

**Vascular territories — recognize the syndrome:**

| Artery | Findings |
|--------|----------|
| **MCA** | Contralateral face/arm > leg weakness + sensory loss; aphasia (dominant) or neglect (non-dominant); homonymous hemianopia |
| **ACA** | Contralateral leg > arm weakness; abulia, urinary incontinence |
| **PCA** | Homonymous hemianopia (with macular sparing); alexia without agraphia (dominant occipital + splenium) |
| **Vertebrobasilar** | Crossed signs (ipsilateral CN deficit + contralateral body), ataxia, vertigo, diplopia, dysarthria, **locked-in** (basilar) |
| **Lacunar** | Pure motor (internal capsule), pure sensory (thalamus), ataxic-hemiparesis, dysarthria-clumsy hand |
| **PICA / lateral medullary (Wallenberg)** | Ipsi face / contra body sensory loss, ipsi Horner, ataxia, vertigo, dysphagia, hoarseness |

**Acute ischemic stroke management:**

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| 1. Time of last known well | Most important question |
| 2. CT head (no contrast) | Rule out bleed |
| 3. Stroke severity | NIHSS |
| 4. Glucose, BP, INR, platelets, troponin, ECG | Differential & workup |
| 5. Reperfusion eligibility | tPA / TNK and / or thrombectomy |
| 6. Admit to stroke unit | DVT prophylaxis, dysphagia screen, monitor |

**IV tPA / tenecteplase eligibility:**

| Criterion |
|-----------|
| Ischemic stroke with measurable deficit |
| Within **4.5 hours** of last known well (extended windows in select patients with imaging mismatch) |
| Age ≥18 |

**Absolute contraindications (selected):**
- Active bleeding / recent major surgery
- Intracranial hemorrhage ever
- Stroke or head trauma <3 mo
- Suspected aortic dissection or active endocarditis
- BP **>185/110** that can't be lowered
- Glucose <50 (treat first)
- Platelet <100, INR >1.7, aPTT abnormal
- DOAC within 48h (unless reversed; idarucizumab for dabigatran)
- Recent intracranial surgery / neoplasm with high bleed risk

**Relative contraindications** — individualized.

**Mechanical thrombectomy:**

| Criterion |
|-----------|
| Large-vessel occlusion (ICA, M1 MCA) |
| Up to 6 hours: standard |
| 6-24 hours: with imaging mismatch (DAWN, DEFUSE-3 criteria — ischemic core small vs penumbra large) |

**Blood pressure management:**

| Setting | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Eligible for tPA | **<185/110** before tPA, then <180/105 × 24h |
| Not eligible (permissive HTN) | Treat only if **>220/120** or end-organ damage; gradual reduction (15% in 24h) |
| Hemorrhagic stroke | SBP **130-140** within hours (INTERACT/ATACH) |

**Secondary prevention:**

| Cause | Therapy |
|-------|---------|
| Non-cardioembolic | **Antiplatelet** (aspirin, clopidogrel, or DAPT short-term post-minor stroke per CHANCE/POINT × 21d), high-intensity statin |
| Cardioembolic (AF, LV thrombus) | **Anticoagulation** (DOAC for AF) |
| Carotid stenosis 70-99% symptomatic | Carotid endarterectomy or stent |
| Symptomatic intracranial stenosis | Aggressive medical Tx (DAPT, statin), maybe stenting selected |
| Patent foramen ovale (PFO) cryptogenic stroke | Closure considered <60y with no other cause |
| Dissection | Anticoagulate or antiplatelet × 3-6 months |

**Hemorrhagic stroke (ICH):**

| Cause | Clue |
|-------|------|
| **Hypertensive (most)** | Deep: putamen, thalamus, pons, cerebellum |
| Cerebral amyloid angiopathy | Lobar, recurrent, elderly |
| AVM, aneurysm | Younger; aneurysm typically SAH |
| Anticoagulant | INR / DOAC |
| Tumor (hemorrhagic met) | Lung, melanoma, RCC, choriocarcinoma, thyroid |

**Acute ICH management:**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| BP | Target **130-140 systolic** within hours |
| Reverse anticoagulation | PCC + vitamin K (warfarin); idarucizumab (dabigatran); andexanet or PCC (factor Xa inhibitors) |
| Surgery | Cerebellar hematoma >3 cm → evacuation; selected lobar |
| ICP management | Head elevated, mannitol/hypertonic saline, sedation, hyperventilation acutely |
| Seizure prophylaxis | Only if seizures occur — not routinely |

**Subarachnoid hemorrhage (SAH):**

| Clue |
|------|
| **"Worst headache of life"** thunderclap |
| Aneurysm rupture (PCom > ACom) |
| Stiff neck, photophobia |
| LP if CT negative — xanthochromia |

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| Detect aneurysm | CTA or DSA |
| Secure aneurysm | **Coiling > clipping** when feasible |
| Prevent vasospasm | **Nimodipine PO × 21 days** (only oral, neuroprotective) |
| Avoid rebleed | BP <140-160 systolic |
| Monitor | Hydrocephalus, vasospasm (TCD, neuro exam), hyponatremia (cerebral salt wasting > SIADH) |

**Stroke mimics — don't tPA:**

| Mimic |
|-------|
| Hypoglycemia (always check) |
| Seizure with Todd's paralysis |
| Migraine with aura |
| Conversion disorder |
| Mass / abscess |
| Wernicke encephalopathy |

**Common gotchas:**
- **CT often normal early** in ischemic stroke (first 6-12h) — that's expected; if CT shows bleed, decision is made
- "Normal" glucose **doesn't rule out hypoglycemic stroke mimic** if symptoms started low
- **Posterior strokes are missed** — vertigo + diplopia + ataxia + dysarthria pattern; head impulse / nystagmus / test of skew (HINTS) helps differentiate central from peripheral vertigo
- After tPA: **no antithrombotics × 24h**, then resume; recheck CT before
- **Dual antiplatelet** (aspirin + clopidogrel or ticagrelor) for **21-30 days only** in minor stroke / TIA, then aspirin alone — long-term DAPT increases bleeding without further benefit
- **Cerebellar stroke** often "deceptively benign" → tonsillar herniation; low threshold to scan dizzy patients

**Rule of thumb:** CT first → ischemic vs hemorrhagic. Time of last known well is everything. tPA <4.5h, thrombectomy LVO up to 24h with mismatch. ICH: BP 130-140, reverse anticoag. SAH: nimodipine + secure aneurysm. Glucose, BP, dysphagia screen always.
