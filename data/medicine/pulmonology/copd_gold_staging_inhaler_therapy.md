### COPD

**Spirometry diagnosis:**

| Criterion | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC | **<0.70** = obstruction |
| Reversibility | Limited (vs asthma which fully reverses) |
| DLCO | ↓ in emphysema (vs preserved in chronic bronchitis) |

**GOLD severity (post-BD FEV1, % predicted):**

| GOLD | FEV1 | Severity |
|------|------|----------|
| 1 | ≥80% | Mild |
| 2 | 50-79% | Moderate |
| 3 | 30-49% | Severe |
| 4 | <30% | Very severe |

**ABCD groups → ABE (2023 simplification):**

| Group | Symptoms (mMRC, CAT) | Exacerbations/year |
|-------|----------------------|--------------------|
| A | Low (mMRC 0-1, CAT <10) | 0-1 (no hosp) |
| B | High | 0-1 (no hosp) |
| **E** | Any | **≥2 moderate or ≥1 hospitalization** |

**Inhaler escalation (GOLD 2023):**

| Group | First step | Next step |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| A | Bronchodilator (SABA or LABA or LAMA) | — |
| B | **LABA + LAMA** | — |
| E | **LABA + LAMA** | + ICS if eosinophils ≥300 |

ICS triple therapy (LABA + LAMA + ICS) for E with eosinophils ≥300 or repeated exacerbations.

**Drug classes:**

| Class | Examples | Mechanism |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| SABA | Albuterol | Quick relief β2-agonist |
| LABA | Salmeterol, formoterol | Long-acting β2 |
| SAMA | Ipratropium | Short-acting muscarinic |
| LAMA | Tiotropium, umeclidinium | Long-acting muscarinic |
| ICS | Budesonide, fluticasone | Inhaled steroid |
| PDE-4 inhibitor | Roflumilast | Severe + chronic bronchitis phenotype |

**ICS — when not to use:**
- COPD without exacerbation history + low eosinophils → ICS adds **pneumonia risk**, no benefit
- Asthma-COPD overlap or eos ≥300 → ICS helpful

**Acute exacerbation (AECOPD) — bundle:**

| Treatment | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| SABA + SAMA nebs | Bronchodilator stack |
| Systemic steroid | **Prednisone 40 mg × 5 days** |
| Antibiotics | If 2 of 3: ↑ dyspnea, ↑ sputum, ↑ purulence; or mech vent |
| **NIV (BiPAP)** | First-line for hypercapnic respiratory failure (pH <7.35, PaCO2 >45) |
| Controlled O2 | Target SpO2 88-92% (avoid hyperoxia → CO2 retention) |
| Smoking cessation | Mortality benefit |

Common AECOPD pathogens: H. flu, Strep pneumo, Moraxella, viral; Pseudomonas in advanced.

**Long-term mortality interventions:**

| Intervention | Effect |
|--------------|--------|
| **Smoking cessation** | Largest single benefit |
| **Long-term O2** if PaO2 ≤55 mmHg or SpO2 ≤88% | Mortality benefit |
| Lung volume reduction (selected upper-lobe emphysema) | Improves function |
| Lung transplant | End-stage |
| Pulmonary rehab | Quality of life, exacerbations |
| Vaccinations (flu, pneumo, COVID, RSV in select) | Reduce infections |

**α1-antitrypsin deficiency — when to suspect:**

| Clue |
|------|
| Emphysema **<45 years old** |
| Lower-lobe predominant |
| Liver disease in same person |
| Family history |
| Never-smoker with COPD |

Test serum α1-AT level → genotype if low.

**Pink puffers vs blue bloaters (heuristic, not diagnostic):**

| Type | Pink puffer (emphysema) | Blue bloater (chronic bronchitis) |
|------|-------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Body habitus | Thin, accessory muscles | Overweight |
| Cyanosis | Late | Early |
| Cor pulmonale | Late | Early |
| DLCO | ↓ | Normal |
| CO2 retention | Less | More |

**Common gotchas:**
- **O2 target 88-92%** in COPD — not 100% (CO2 retention via Haldane effect, V/Q mismatch)
- ICS in pure COPD without indication → ↑ pneumonia
- Steroid burst is short — **5 days** of prednisone, no taper
- Don't miss **α1-AT** in young / non-smoker / lower-lobe emphysema
- Pulm rehab is high-yield and underutilized

**Rule of thumb:** COPD = post-BD FEV1/FVC <0.70. Group E = 2+ exacerbations or hospitalization. LABA+LAMA core; add ICS only if eos high or asthma overlap. Smoking cessation + LTO2 are the only mortality-improving interventions besides transplant.
