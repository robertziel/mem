### Pneumonia

**Three settings:**

| Type | Definition | Common pathogens |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| CAP | Community onset (or <48h after hospitalization) | Strep pneumo, Mycoplasma, Chlamydia, H. flu, Legionella, viruses |
| HAP | Onset ≥48h after admission | Pseudomonas, MRSA, Gram-negatives, Enterobacter |
| VAP | Onset ≥48h on mechanical ventilation | Pseudomonas, MRSA, Acinetobacter, ESBL |

**CAP severity — CURB-65 (each = 1 point):**

| Letter | Item |
|--------|------|
| C | Confusion |
| U | Urea (BUN) >19 mg/dL (>7 mmol/L) |
| R | RR ≥30 |
| B | BP <90 sys or ≤60 dia |
| 65 | Age ≥65 |

| Score | Site |
|-------|------|
| 0-1 | Outpatient |
| 2 | Consider inpatient |
| 3-5 | Inpatient ± ICU |

PSI (Pneumonia Severity Index) is more granular but more complex.

**ATS/IDSA severe CAP** — at least 1 major or 3 minor:

| Major | Minor |
|-------|-------|
| Septic shock requiring vasopressors | RR ≥30, PaO2/FiO2 ≤250, multilobar infiltrates, confusion, BUN ≥20, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia, hypothermia, hypotension requiring fluids |
| Mech ventilation | |

**CAP empiric therapy (IDSA/ATS 2019):**

| Setting | Regimen |
|---------|---------|
| Outpatient, no comorbidity | Amoxicillin or doxycycline or macrolide (if local resistance <25%) |
| Outpatient with comorbidity | β-lactam (amox-clav, cefpodoxime, cefuroxime) **+ macrolide or doxy**, OR resp fluoroquinolone (levo, moxi) |
| Inpatient (non-severe) | β-lactam + macrolide, OR resp FQ |
| Inpatient severe / ICU | β-lactam + (macrolide or FQ); add MRSA / Pseudomonas coverage if RFs |

**MRSA / Pseudomonas risk → broaden coverage:**

| RFs |
|-----|
| Prior MRSA / Pseudomonas isolation |
| Recent IV antibiotics in 90 d |
| Recent hospitalization in 90 d |
| Severe CAP (esp. cavitary) |
| Structural lung disease (CF, bronchiectasis) |

Add: **vancomycin or linezolid** (MRSA) + **antipseudomonal β-lactam** (pip-tazo, cefepime, meropenem, ceftazidime) ± aminoglycoside.

**HAP / VAP empiric therapy:**

| Coverage needed | Drugs |
|-----------------|-------|
| Standard | Cefepime, levofloxacin, or pip-tazo |
| MRSA risk | Add **vanc or linezolid** |
| Multi-drug-resistant Pseudomonas / G− | Two anti-pseudomonal agents from different classes |

De-escalate after culture/sensitivity (Day 2-3).

**Atypical pneumonia clues:**

| Pathogen | Clue |
|----------|------|
| Mycoplasma | Young adult, dry cough, bullous myringitis, cold agglutinins |
| Chlamydophila pneumoniae | Pharyngitis, bronchitis, hoarseness |
| Legionella | Hyponatremia, GI symptoms, ↑LFTs, water source/hotel/travel; urine antigen |
| Coxiella burnetii (Q fever) | Animal/birthing exposure |
| Chlamydia psittaci | Bird exposure |

**CXR clues (pattern-based):**

| Pattern | Likely cause |
|---------|--------------|
| Lobar consolidation | Strep pneumo |
| Multifocal patchy | Atypicals, viral |
| Cavitary upper lobe | TB, Klebsiella, anaerobes (aspiration) |
| Bilateral interstitial | PJP, viral, atypicals |
| Lung abscess (cavity + air-fluid level) | Anaerobes (aspiration) |
| Currant jelly sputum | Klebsiella |
| Rust-colored sputum | Strep pneumo |

**Aspiration pneumonia / pneumonitis:**

| Type | Cause | Treatment |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Pneumonitis (chemical) | Acid injury — first hours | Supportive, no antibiotics |
| Pneumonia (infectious, days) | Anaerobes ± aerobes | Amox-clav, ampicillin-sulbactam, clindamycin (alternative) |

Common aspiration sites: dependent (RUL if supine, RLL if upright), right lung in general (more vertical RMB).

**Special populations:**

| Population | Pathogen |
|------------|----------|
| HIV CD4 <200 | **PJP** — bilateral interstitial, ↑LDH, treat with TMP-SMX + steroids if PaO2 <70 |
| Post-influenza | Strep pneumo, Staph aureus, H. flu |
| CF / bronchiectasis | Pseudomonas, Burkholderia, Staph |
| Alcoholics | Klebsiella, anaerobes, TB |
| IVDU | Staph (right-sided IE → septic emboli) |
| Recent travel SW US | Coccidioides |
| Birds | Chlamydia psittaci, histoplasmosis |

**Duration:**

| Setting | Course |
|---------|--------|
| CAP outpatient | 5 days (if afebrile + clinically improving by Day 3) |
| CAP inpatient | 5-7 days |
| Severe / structural / immunocompromised | Longer, individualized |
| HAP/VAP | 7 days typical |
| Lung abscess | Weeks |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Don't add anaerobic coverage** for routine aspiration in alert non-dental patient — most need standard CAP regimen
- **Influenza → Staph pneumonia** — necrotizing, severe (use anti-MRSA)
- **Legionella** — order urine antigen with severe CAP, hyponatremia, or ↑LFTs
- Procalcitonin can guide stop / start of antibiotics
- **PJP without HIV** (transplant, biologics) — high mortality, low LDH less reliable

**Rule of thumb:** Setting → empirical regimen → de-escalate. CURB-65 triages site of care. Atypicals → β-lactam + macrolide / doxy. Always cover MRSA + Pseudomonas in severe CAP with risk factors.
