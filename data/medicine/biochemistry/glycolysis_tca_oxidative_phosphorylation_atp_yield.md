### Glycolysis, TCA, Oxidative Phosphorylation

**Pathway overview:**

| Stage | Location | Input | Output (per glucose) |
|-------|----------|-------|----------------------|
| Glycolysis | Cytoplasm | Glucose | 2 pyruvate, 2 ATP, 2 NADH |
| Pyruvate → Acetyl-CoA | Mitochondrial matrix | 2 pyruvate | 2 Acetyl-CoA, 2 NADH, 2 CO2 |
| TCA (Krebs) | Mitochondrial matrix | 2 Acetyl-CoA | 6 NADH, 2 FADH2, 2 GTP, 4 CO2 |
| Electron transport / OxPhos | Inner mito membrane | NADH, FADH2, O2 | ATP (chemiosmosis), H2O |

**ATP yield per glucose (aerobic):**

| Source | Yield |
|--------|-------|
| Glycolysis (substrate-level) | 2 ATP |
| TCA (substrate-level GTP) | 2 ATP |
| 10 NADH × 2.5 ATP | 25 ATP |
| 2 FADH2 × 1.5 ATP | 3 ATP |
| **Total** | **~32 ATP** (older texts: 36-38) |

**Glycolysis regulation (rate-limiting steps):**

| Enzyme | Activators | Inhibitors |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| Hexokinase / Glucokinase (liver) | — | G6P (HK), insulin promotes GK |
| **PFK-1** (committed step) | AMP, F2,6BP, ↑[Pi] | ATP, citrate, low pH |
| Pyruvate kinase | F1,6BP (feed-forward), insulin | ATP, alanine, glucagon (P) |

**TCA cycle highlights:**

| Step | Enzyme | Note |
|------|--------|------|
| Acetyl-CoA + OAA → citrate | Citrate synthase | Inhibited by citrate, ATP |
| Isocitrate → α-KG | Isocitrate dehydrogenase | Rate-limiting, NAD⁺-dependent |
| α-KG → succinyl-CoA | α-KG dehydrogenase | Needs B1, B2, B3, B5, lipoate (same cofactors as PDH) |
| Succinate → fumarate | Succinate dehydrogenase | Complex II; FAD-linked |
| Malate → OAA | Malate dehydrogenase | Generates NADH |

**Electron transport chain:**

| Complex | Donates electrons | Pumps H⁺? |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| I (NADH dehydrogenase) | NADH → CoQ | Yes |
| II (Succinate dehydrogenase) | FADH2 → CoQ | No |
| III (Cytochrome bc1) | CoQ → Cyt c | Yes |
| IV (Cytochrome c oxidase) | Cyt c → O2 | Yes |
| V (ATP synthase) | H⁺ gradient | Synthesizes ATP |

**Common inhibitors / uncouplers:**

| Agent | Site | Effect |
|-------|------|--------|
| Rotenone | Complex I | Blocks NADH oxidation |
| Antimycin A | Complex III | — |
| Cyanide, CO, azide | Complex IV | Histotoxic hypoxia |
| Oligomycin | Complex V | Blocks ATP synthase |
| 2,4-DNP, aspirin (toxic dose), thermogenin (UCP1) | Inner membrane | Uncoupler — H⁺ leak, heat, no ATP |

**Anaerobic fate:** pyruvate → lactate (via LDH, regenerates NAD⁺). Lactate accumulates → metabolic acidosis.

**Common gotchas:**
- **B vitamins for PDH/α-KG-DH**: B1 (thiamine), B2, B3, B5, lipoate — thiamine deficiency → ↑pyruvate, ↑lactate, Wernicke
- Cyanide/CO bind Complex IV → cells can't use O2 even if PaO2 is normal → bright-red venous blood, severe lactic acidosis
- DNP (uncoupler) → fever, weight loss, lactic acidosis (deadly diet pill)
- Glycolysis still runs anaerobically (the only ATP source for RBCs — they have no mitochondria)

**Rule of thumb:** Aerobic glucose → 32 ATP. PFK-1 controls glycolysis, isocitrate-DH paces TCA, Complex IV is the O2 acceptor — block it (CN/CO) and the chain stalls instantly.
