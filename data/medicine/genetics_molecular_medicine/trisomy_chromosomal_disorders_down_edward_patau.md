### Trisomies and Major Chromosomal Disorders

**Autosomal trisomies (compatible with live birth):**

| Trisomy | Name | Frequency | Survival | Hallmarks |
|---------|------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 21 | Down | 1:700 | Adulthood | Flat facies, epicanthal folds, single palmar crease, hypotonia, AVSD/VSD, duodenal atresia, ↑ALL/AML, early Alzheimer |
| 18 | Edwards | 1:6000 | <1 year | Clenched fists w/ overlapping fingers, rocker-bottom feet, micrognathia, prominent occiput, VSD |
| 13 | Patau | 1:15000 | <1 year | Cleft lip/palate, holoprosencephaly, polydactyly, microphthalmia, cutis aplasia |

Mnemonic: D**rinking** (21), **E**lection (18 — voting), **P**uberty (13).

**Mechanisms of trisomy:**
- Most: meiotic nondisjunction (95% of Down — maternal age-related)
- ~4% Down: **Robertsonian translocation** (often 14;21) — recurrence risk much higher → karyotype both parents
- ~1% Down: mosaicism (variable phenotype)

**Sex chromosome aneuploidies:**

| Karyotype | Name | Phenotype |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| 45,X | Turner | Short, webbed neck, shield chest, **bicuspid AV/coarctation**, streak gonads, primary amenorrhea, lymphedema, normal IQ |
| 47,XXY | Klinefelter | Tall, gynecomastia, small testes, ↓testosterone, ↑LH/FSH, infertility, learning issues |
| 47,XXX | Triple X | Often asymptomatic, mild learning issues, tall |
| 47,XYY | Jacobs | Tall, normal fertility, mild behavioral/learning |

**Microdeletion / contiguous gene syndromes:**

| Syndrome | Region | Features |
|----------|--------|----------|
| DiGeorge (22q11.2) | "CATCH-22": Cardiac (truncus, ToF), Abnormal facies, Thymic aplasia (T-cell↓), Cleft palate, Hypocalcemia |
| Williams (7q11.23) | Elfin facies, supravalvular AS, hypercalcemia, "cocktail party" personality |
| Cri-du-chat (5p−) | High-pitched cry, microcephaly, ID, heart defects |
| Wolf-Hirschhorn (4p−) | "Greek warrior helmet" facies, seizures |
| WAGR (11p13) | Wilms tumor, Aniridia, GU anomalies, mental Retardation |
| Prader-Willi (paternal 15q11-13) | Hyperphagia, obesity, hypotonia, hypogonadism, ID |
| Angelman (maternal 15q11-13) | "Happy puppet" — laughter, ataxia, seizures, severe ID |
| Beckwith-Wiedemann (11p15) | Macroglossia, omphalocele, hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia, ↑Wilms/HB |

**Imprinting recap:**

| Syndrome | Loss of | Mechanism |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Prader-Willi | **Paternal** 15q | Deletion (75%) or maternal UPD (25%) |
| Angelman | **Maternal** 15q | Deletion (70%), paternal UPD, UBE3A mutation |

Same locus, opposite parental origin → different syndromes.

**Prenatal screening (first vs second trimester):**

| Test | Down | Edwards | Patau | Open NTD |
|------|------|---------|-------|----------|
| First trimester (PAPP-A, β-hCG, NT) | ↓PAPP-A, ↑β-hCG, ↑NT | ↓ all | ↓ all | — |
| Quad screen (AFP, hCG, estriol, inhibin A) | ↓AFP, ↑hCG, ↓estriol, ↑inhibin | All ↓ | — | ↑AFP |
| Cell-free fetal DNA (NIPT) | High sensitivity from 10wk | Yes | Yes | — |

Definitive: **CVS** (10-13wk, chorionic villus) or **amnio** (15+wk, karyotype/microarray).

**Common gotchas:**
- Maternal age is the strongest risk for trisomy 21 — but **most Down babies are born to younger mothers** (more births overall)
- Recurrence risk in Robertsonian Down is high → karyotype every parent of a child with Down
- Turner is the **only viable monosomy** (45,X)
- Don't confuse **Klinefelter (47,XXY) — primary hypogonadism** with **Kallmann (hypothalamic, normal karyotype)**
- DiGeorge → check **calcium and CD3+ T cells** in any neonate with cardiac anomaly + odd facies

**Rule of thumb:** Down 21 = ALL/Alzheimer/AVSD. Edward 18 = clenched/rocker. Patau 13 = cleft + holoprosencephaly. Turner = short + heart + ovarian failure. Klinefelter = tall + small testes + gynecomastia. 22q = CATCH-22.
