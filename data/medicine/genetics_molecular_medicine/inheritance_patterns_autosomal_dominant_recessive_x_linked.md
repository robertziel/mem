### Inheritance Patterns

**Pedigree pattern recognition:**

| Pattern | Affected | Skip generations? | Male:Female | Father→Son |
|---------|----------|-------------------|-------------|-----------|
| Autosomal dominant | ~50% offspring | No | 1:1 | Yes |
| Autosomal recessive | Both parents carriers; ~25% | Yes | 1:1 | Yes |
| X-linked dominant | All daughters of affected ♂; ½ kids of affected ♀ | No | More ♀ (mild) | **No** (key) |
| X-linked recessive | ♂ from carrier ♀; ♀ rare | Yes | Mostly ♂ | **No** |
| Mitochondrial | All kids of affected ♀; **none** from ♂ | No | Equal | **No** |

**Key examples:**

| Pattern | Diseases |
|---------|----------|
| **AD** | Huntington, Marfan, NF1/NF2, ADPKD, FAP, HOCM, achondroplasia, hereditary spherocytosis, vWD, FH (LDL-R) |
| **AR** | Cystic fibrosis, sickle cell, β-thalassemia, PKU, albinism, Wilson, hemochromatosis, ARPKD, Tay-Sachs, Friedreich, Gaucher |
| **XR** | DMD/BMD, hemophilia A & B, G6PD, Lesch-Nyhan, Wiskott-Aldrich, Fabry, Bruton agammaglobulinemia, color blindness, ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency |
| **XD** | Hypophosphatemic rickets, Rett (♂ usually lethal), fragile X, incontinentia pigmenti |
| **Mitochondrial** | LHON, MELAS, MERRF, Leigh, Kearns-Sayre |

**Clues to inheritance from a vignette:**

| Clue | Likely pattern |
|------|----------------|
| Affected in every generation, both sexes, father→son | AD |
| Two unaffected parents, affected sibling(s), consanguinity | AR |
| Affected males through carrier mothers, no father→son | XR |
| Affected mother passes to **all** children, never father | Mitochondrial |
| Severity varies wildly within family | Mitochondrial (heteroplasmy) or AD with variable expressivity |

**Special concepts:**

| Concept | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Penetrance | % of genotypes that show phenotype | BRCA1 ~70% |
| Variable expressivity | Same genotype, different severity | NF1 |
| Anticipation | Earlier/severer in successive generations | Trinucleotide repeats (Huntington, fragile X, myotonic dystrophy) |
| Imprinting | Only maternal/paternal allele expressed | Prader-Willi (paternal del), Angelman (maternal del) — both 15q |
| Mosaicism | Two cell lines from one zygote | McCune-Albright, somatic mutations |
| Uniparental disomy | Both copies from one parent | Some Prader-Willi/Angelman |
| Heteroplasmy | Variable proportion of mutant mtDNA | Mitochondrial disease severity |
| Loss of heterozygosity | Tumor suppressor "second hit" | Retinoblastoma (Knudson 2-hit) |

**Trinucleotide repeat diseases:**

| Disease | Repeat | Region | Inheritance |
|---------|--------|--------|-------------|
| Huntington | CAG | Coding (HTT) | AD |
| Myotonic dystrophy | CTG | 3' UTR (DMPK) | AD |
| Fragile X | CGG | 5' UTR (FMR1) | XD (functionally) |
| Friedreich ataxia | GAA | Intronic (FXN) | AR |

"**Try Hunting For My Fragile Cage**" — CAG/CTG/CGG/GAA.

**Common gotchas:**
- **AD with father→son transmission** → rules out X-linked
- **Two unaffected parents → affected child** → AR (or new mutation, or imprinting)
- **All daughters of an affected man affected** → think XD
- **Mother → all kids** with **no father transmission** → mitochondrial
- 50% of NF1 cases are **new mutations** (high mutation rate); pedigree may look noncontributory

**Rule of thumb:** Father→son rules out X-linked. Skipping a generation rules out AD. Affected mother → all children = mitochondrial. Look for consanguinity for AR.
