### Viral Classification

**Top-level split:**

| Genome | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| dsDNA | Herpes, Adeno, Papilloma, Polyoma, Pox, Hepadna |
| ssDNA | Parvo (B19) |
| dsRNA | Reo, Rotavirus |
| (+)ssRNA | Picorna, Calici, Hepe, Flavi, Toga, Retro, Corona |
| (−)ssRNA | Orthomyxo (flu), Paramyxo, Rhabdo, Filo, Bunya, Arena |

**Mnemonic for DNA viruses (HHAPPPPy):** Herpes, Hepadna, Adeno, Pox, Parvo, Papilloma, Polyoma. All dsDNA except **Parvo** (ss); all replicate in nucleus except **Pox** (cytoplasm — own polymerase).

**Envelope vs naked:**

| Type | Examples | Clue |
|------|----------|------|
| Enveloped | Herpes, Hepadna, Pox, all (−)ssRNA, Retro, Corona, Toga, Flavi, Hepe, HCV | Sensitive to detergents, drying |
| Naked | **Parvo, Papilloma, Polyoma, Adeno** (DNA); **Picorna, Calici, Reo, Hepe** (RNA) | Survive in stool, more resistant |

Mnemonic naked DNA: **PAPP** (Parvo, Adeno, Papilloma, Polyoma). Naked RNA: **CPR + Hep E** (Calici, Picorna, Reo, HepE).

**Hepatitis viruses:**

| Virus | Family | Genome | Transmission | Chronic? |
|-------|--------|--------|--------------|----------|
| HAV | Picornavirus | (+)ssRNA | Fecal-oral | No |
| HBV | Hepadnavirus | partial dsDNA | Blood, sex, vertical | Yes (5-10% adult) |
| HCV | Flavivirus | (+)ssRNA | Blood (IVDU) | **Yes (~80%)** |
| HDV | Deltavirus | (−)ssRNA | Co/super-infect HBV | Worsens HBV |
| HEV | Hepevirus | (+)ssRNA | Fecal-oral, **fatal in pregnancy** | No |

**RNA virus highlights:**

| Family | Members | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Picornavirus | Polio, Coxsackie, Echo, Rhino, HAV | Naked, (+)ssRNA |
| Flavivirus | Dengue, Yellow fever, West Nile, Zika, HCV, JE | Enveloped, (+)ssRNA |
| Togavirus | Rubella, EEE/WEE, Chikungunya | — |
| Coronavirus | SARS, MERS, SARS-CoV-2 | Largest RNA genome |
| Orthomyxovirus | Influenza A/B/C | **Segmented**, antigenic drift/shift |
| Paramyxovirus | Measles, mumps, parainfluenza, RSV | Non-segmented, F protein |
| Rhabdovirus | Rabies | Bullet-shaped, Negri bodies |
| Reovirus | Rotavirus | dsRNA, segmented, infantile diarrhea |
| Retrovirus | HIV, HTLV | Reverse transcriptase |

**Antigenic drift vs shift (influenza):**

| Mechanism | Cause | Result |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Drift | Point mutations | Annual epidemics, partial cross-immunity |
| Shift | Segment reassortment (multi-host) | **Pandemic** — no immunity |

**Common viruses by clinical clue:**

| Clue | Virus |
|------|-------|
| "Slapped cheek" rash | Parvovirus B19 |
| Koplik spots → rash | Measles |
| "3-day rash" after fever in toddler | HHV-6 (roseola) |
| Vesicles on red base, dermatomal | VZV (shingles) |
| Heterophile + atypical lymphs | EBV (mono) |
| Heterophile − mono-like, owl's eye | CMV |
| Bullet-shaped, encephalitis after dog bite | Rabies |
| Negri bodies in hippocampus | Rabies |
| Owl's eye nuclear inclusions | CMV |
| Cowdry A bodies | HSV, VZV |

**Common gotchas:**
- HBV: partial dsDNA but **uses reverse transcriptase** → why nucleoside analogs work
- HDV requires HBsAg coat → only co/super-infection
- Pox: only DNA virus replicating in **cytoplasm** (carries own DNA polymerase)
- HEV: 20% mortality in pregnancy (mechanism unknown)
- Influenza shift requires multi-segment reassortment in dual-host (e.g., pig)

**Rule of thumb:** Genome (DNA/RNA, ds/ss, ±sense) → enveloped/naked → family → clinical fingerprint. Memorize the small lists (HHAPPPPy DNA, naked PAPP, segmented = flu/rota/bunya/arena).
