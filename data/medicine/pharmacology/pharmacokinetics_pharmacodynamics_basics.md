### Pharmacokinetics & Pharmacodynamics

**PK = "what the body does to the drug" (ADME):**

| Phase | Determinants |
|-------|-------------|
| **A**bsorption | Bioavailability (F), route, first-pass |
| **D**istribution | Vd, protein binding |
| **M**etabolism | CYP450 (Phase I) → conjugation (Phase II) |
| **E**limination | CL, half-life, route (renal/hepatic) |

**PD = "what the drug does to the body":**

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Affinity | How well drug binds receptor (Kd) |
| Efficacy | Maximum response (Emax) |
| Potency | Dose to reach 50% effect (EC50) — **lower = more potent** |
| Therapeutic index | LD50 / ED50 — higher = safer |

**Volume of distribution (Vd):**

| Vd value | Compartment | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| 3-5 L | Plasma (protein-bound) | Heparin, warfarin, aspirin |
| 14-18 L | Extracellular fluid (water-soluble) | Aminoglycosides |
| 41 L | Total body water | Ethanol |
| >41 L | Tissue-bound (lipophilic, ion-trapped) | Amiodarone, digoxin, TCAs, chloroquine |

**High Vd → poor dialysis clearance** (drug is sequestered in tissue, not blood).

**Half-life and clearance:**

t½ = (0.693 × Vd) / CL

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| ↑ Vd | ↑ t½ (sequestered) |
| ↑ CL | ↓ t½ (eliminated faster) |
| 4 t½ | ~94% steady state |
| 4-5 t½ | ~97% eliminated |

**Loading vs maintenance dose:**

| Goal | Formula |
|------|---------|
| Loading dose | Vd × Cp(target) / F |
| Maintenance dose | CL × Cp(target) / F (per dosing interval) |

Loading depends on **Vd**; maintenance depends on **CL**.

**Zero vs first-order kinetics:**

| Feature | Zero-order | First-order |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Rate | Constant amount/time | Constant fraction/time |
| Causes | Saturated enzyme | Most drugs |
| Examples | **PEA**: Phenytoin (high), Ethanol, Aspirin (toxic) | Most drugs |
| t½ | Variable | Constant |

**Phase I vs Phase II metabolism:**

| Phase | Reaction | Result |
|-------|----------|--------|
| I | Oxidation (CYP450), reduction, hydrolysis | Often active metabolite |
| II | Conjugation (glucuronide, sulfate, acetyl, glycine, glutathione) | Inactive, water-soluble, excreted |

**Geriatric tip:** Phase I declines with age; Phase II preserved → favor lorazepam/oxazepam/temazepam (LOT — Phase II only) over diazepam in elderly.

**CYP450 inducers vs inhibitors (drug-drug interactions):**

| Inducers (↓ levels) | Inhibitors (↑ levels) |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| **CRAP GPS**: Carbamazepine, Rifampin, Alcohol (chronic), Phenytoin, Griseofulvin, Phenobarb, St John's wort | **MACROS GIQ**: Macrolides, Amiodarone, Cimetidine, Ritonavir, Omeprazole, Sulfonamides, Grapefruit, Isoniazid, Quinidine, ketoconazole |

**Receptor pharmacology:**

| Type | Acts on signaling | Examples |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| Full agonist | 100% Emax | Morphine |
| Partial agonist | <100% Emax (even at full receptor occupancy) | Buprenorphine, varenicline |
| Inverse agonist | Negative response | β-carbolines |
| Competitive antagonist | Surmountable (right shift) | Naloxone |
| Non-competitive antagonist | Unsurmountable (↓ Emax) | Aspirin on COX |

**Common gotchas:**
- **High Vd ≠ "lots of drug"** — it means drug is in tissue, plasma levels low; large doses needed and dialysis useless
- **Phenytoin, ethanol, aspirin** at toxic levels switch to **zero-order** — small dose ↑ → large level ↑
- **Bioavailability** of oral drug ≤ 1; IV drug F = 1.0
- **Grapefruit juice** inhibits intestinal CYP3A4 → ↑ levels of statins, CCBs, cyclosporine
- **Acidic urine** traps weak bases (e.g., amphetamine, TCA) — **alkalinize urine** to enhance elimination of weak acids (aspirin, phenobarb)

**Rule of thumb:** Loading depends on Vd, maintenance on CL. Zero-order = saturated enzyme (PEA). Inducers ↓ levels (CRAP GPS), inhibitors ↑ levels. Steady state in 4-5 half-lives.
