### Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)

**Clinical hallmarks:**

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Distribution | **Symmetric, small joints**: MCPs, PIPs, wrists, MTPs; spares **DIP** (vs OA) |
| Morning stiffness | **>1 hour**, improves with use |
| Constitutional | Fatigue, low-grade fever, weight loss |
| Onset | Insidious (more common) or acute |
| Female:male | 3:1 |

**ACR/EULAR 2010 classification (for early diagnosis, sum ≥6/10):**

| Category | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Joints (1 large = 0; 2-10 large = 1; 1-3 small = 2; 4-10 small = 3; >10 incl 1+ small = 5) | 0-5 |
| Serology (RF or anti-CCP: low ↑ = 2; high ↑ = 3) | 0-3 |
| Acute-phase reactants (↑ESR or CRP) | 0-1 |
| Symptom duration ≥6 weeks | 0-1 |

Score ≥6 → definite RA.

**Serology:**

| Test | Sensitivity | Specificity |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **RF** | ~70% | ~80% (also seen in HCV, Sjögren, SLE, healthy elderly) |
| **Anti-CCP (anti-citrullinated)** | ~70% | **~95%** — also predicts erosive disease and extra-articular manifestations |

**Imaging:**

| Modality | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| Plain film (early) | Often normal; soft-tissue swelling, periarticular osteopenia |
| Plain film (late) | **Marginal erosions**, joint space narrowing, ulnar deviation, swan neck, boutonnière |
| **Ultrasound / MRI** | Earlier detection of synovitis, erosions |

**Joint deformities of RA (chronic):**

| Deformity | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Swan neck | DIP flexion + PIP hyperextension |
| Boutonnière | DIP hyperextension + PIP flexion |
| Ulnar deviation | At MCPs |
| Z-deformity of thumb | — |
| Atlantoaxial subluxation | Cervical spine — risk during intubation |

**Extra-articular manifestations:**

| System | Manifestation |
|--------|---------------|
| Eyes | **Keratoconjunctivitis sicca, scleritis, episcleritis** |
| Lungs | **Interstitial lung disease**, pleural effusion (low glucose), rheumatoid nodules, Caplan syndrome |
| Heart | Pericarditis, ↑ASCVD risk |
| Skin | Rheumatoid nodules (extensor surfaces), vasculitis |
| Hematologic | Anemia of chronic disease, **Felty syndrome** (RA + splenomegaly + neutropenia), **lymphoma** ↑ |
| Renal | Amyloidosis, NSAID-related, drug nephrotoxicity |
| Neuro | Cervical myelopathy (atlantoaxial subluxation), entrapment (carpal tunnel), mononeuritis |

**Treatment ladder (treat-to-target — remission or low disease activity):**

| Step | Drug |
|------|------|
| **First-line** | **Methotrexate** (start within 12 weeks of diagnosis); add folic acid; bridge with low-dose prednisone short term |
| Add or switch | Hydroxychloroquine (mild), sulfasalazine, leflunomide; combination cs DMARDs (triple therapy) |
| Inadequate response | **Biologic DMARD**: TNF-α (adalimumab, etanercept, infliximab, certolizumab, golimumab) **or** non-TNF (abatacept, rituximab, tocilizumab/sarilumab IL-6) |
| Refractory | JAK inhibitors (tofacitinib, baricitinib, upadacitinib) — **boxed warnings**: VTE, cardiovascular, malignancy, infection in older / smokers |

**MTX requirements / monitoring:**

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Dose | 7.5-25 mg weekly PO or SC |
| Folic acid | 1 mg daily (reduces side effects) |
| Monitor | CBC, LFTs, Cr — baseline, every 1-3 mo |
| Avoid | Pregnancy (teratogen — wash out 3 mo before conception, both sexes), severe liver disease, eGFR <30, alcohol excess |
| Counsel | Pneumonitis (acute), cytopenias, oral ulcers, GI |

**Pre-biologic screen:** PPD or IGRA, HBV/HCV/HIV, CXR, vaccinations.

**Live vaccines** (zoster, MMR, yellow fever) — give before starting biologics; avoid during.

**RA in pregnancy:**

| Drug | Pregnancy |
|------|-----------|
| Hydroxychloroquine | Compatible |
| Sulfasalazine | Compatible (with folate) |
| **Certolizumab** | Compatible (no placental transfer) |
| Other TNF | Generally compatible 1st-2nd trimester |
| MTX, leflunomide | **Contraindicated** — wash out |
| JAK inhibitors | Avoid |

**Differentiating RA from mimics:**

| Disease | Clue |
|---------|------|
| OA | DIP > PIP, weight-bearing joints, stiffness <30 min, bony osteophytes (Heberden, Bouchard) |
| Psoriatic arthritis | Asymmetric, DIP, dactylitis, nail changes, skin |
| SLE | Joint pain non-erosive, multi-system |
| Polymyalgia rheumatica | Older, shoulder/hip girdle, ESR very high, prompt steroid response |
| Reactive arthritis | Asymmetric oligo, post-infection, conjunctivitis, urethritis |
| Crystal | Acute, monoarticular, joint aspiration |

**Common gotchas:**
- **Cervical spine instability** — get imaging before intubation in long-standing RA
- **Methotrexate-induced pneumonitis** — fever, cough, dyspnea; stop drug
- **Felty's** = RA + splenomegaly + neutropenia → infection risk; rituximab
- **Lymphoma risk** in RA is higher than baseline (and higher with disease activity than with biologics)
- **Live zoster vaccine** before biologics; recombinant Shingrix is preferred and safe during
- Anti-CCP is more **specific**; RF can be falsely positive (HCV common reason)

**Rule of thumb:** Symmetric small joint + morning stiffness >1h + RF/CCP = RA. Treat-to-target with MTX first, add biologic if inadequate. Always screen for cervical instability, ILD, eye disease, NSAID-renal/GI, vaccines, infection, lymphoma.
