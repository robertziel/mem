### Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)

**Three flavors:**

| Type | ECG | Troponin | Pathology |
|------|-----|----------|-----------|
| Unstable angina (UA) | Normal or nonspecific | **Normal** | Plaque, partial thrombus, no infarct |
| NSTEMI | ST depression / T-wave inversion | **Elevated** | Subendocardial infarct |
| STEMI | ST elevation in ≥2 contiguous leads, new LBBB | **Elevated** | Transmural infarct, full occlusion |

**STEMI ECG patterns:**

| Leads with ST↑ | Territory | Coronary artery |
|----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| V1-V2 | Septal | Proximal LAD |
| V3-V4 | Anterior | LAD |
| V5-V6 | Lateral | LCx or distal LAD |
| I, aVL, V5-V6 | High lateral | LCx |
| II, III, aVF | Inferior | RCA (90%) or LCx |
| V7-V9, ST↓ V1-V3 (reciprocal) | Posterior | RCA / LCx |
| V4R | Right ventricle | Proximal RCA |

**Inferior MI = check RV** (V4R) — if RV involved, **avoid nitrates and morphine** (drop preload, hypotension).

**Initial management ("MONA-BASH" — though guidelines have evolved):**

| Drug | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **A**spirin 162-325 mg chewed | Always — single biggest mortality benefit |
| P2Y12 inhibitor | Ticagrelor / prasugrel / clopidogrel |
| **N**itrates | Sublingual, then IV; avoid in RV MI, sildenafil/tadalafil within 24-48h |
| Morphine | Pain — but **may worsen outcomes** (slows P2Y12 absorption) |
| Anticoagulation | Heparin (UFH or LMWH) |
| **B**eta blocker | Within 24h if no shock, HR<100, SBP>100 |
| **S**tatin | High intensity (atorvastatin 80) |
| ACEi | Within 24h, especially anterior MI / EF<40 |
| Oxygen | Only if SpO2<90% |

**Reperfusion strategy:**

| Setting | Strategy |
|---------|----------|
| STEMI | **PCI within 90 min** (door-to-balloon) — preferred. If transfer, target 120 min total. |
| STEMI, no PCI <120 min | **Fibrinolysis within 30 min** (door-to-needle) if no contraindications |
| NSTEMI / UA — high risk (TIMI ≥3, ↑trop, dynamic ECG, hemodynamic instability) | Early invasive (PCI <24h) |
| NSTEMI / UA — low risk | Conservative + risk stratification |

**Fibrinolysis contraindications (absolute):**
- Prior intracranial hemorrhage
- Known intracranial vascular lesion / neoplasm
- Ischemic stroke <3 mo
- Active bleeding / dissection
- Closed-head trauma <3 mo

**Troponin kinetics:**

| Marker | Rises | Peaks | Returns |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|
| Troponin (high-sens) | 1-3h | 12-24h | 7-14d |
| CK-MB | 4-6h | 24h | 48-72h (useful for **re-infarction**) |

**Post-MI complications by timing:**

| Time | Complication |
|------|--------------|
| 0-24h | Arrhythmia (V-fib most common cause of death pre-hospital), cardiogenic shock |
| 1-3 d | Pericarditis (early, fibrinous) |
| 3-7 d | Free wall rupture → tamponade; papillary muscle rupture → acute MR; septal rupture → VSD |
| 5-14 d | LV aneurysm → persistent ST elevation, mural thrombus |
| 2-10 wk | **Dressler syndrome** (autoimmune pericarditis, fever, ↑ESR) |

**Long-term post-MI meds (mortality benefit):**
- ASA + P2Y12 (DAPT for 1 year, then ASA alone)
- Statin (high-intensity)
- ACEi/ARB (especially if EF<40)
- β-blocker
- Mineralocorticoid antagonist (eplerenone) if EF<40 + HF/DM
- Lifestyle: smoking cessation, exercise, BP/lipid/glucose control

**Common gotchas:**
- **Inferior MI + hypotension** → suspect RV infarct; treat with **fluids**, avoid nitrates/morphine
- **New LBBB** in chest pain context = **STEMI equivalent** (Sgarbossa criteria can refine)
- **Wellens sign** (deep symmetric T-wave inversions V2-V3) = **critical proximal LAD** lesion — pre-infarction pattern
- **De Winter T-waves** (upsloping ST↓ + tall symmetric T waves V1-V6) = LAD occlusion equivalent
- **Posterior MI** is missed without V7-V9 leads — look for tall R waves and ST↓ in V1-V3
- Troponin can elevate without ACS: PE, sepsis, HF, myocarditis, renal failure, demand ischemia

**Rule of thumb:** STEMI → PCI <90 min. NSTEMI/UA → DAPT + heparin + risk-stratify. Ant + RV MI changes drug choices. Post-MI mortality drugs: ASA, P2Y12, statin, ACEi, β-blocker, ± MRA.
