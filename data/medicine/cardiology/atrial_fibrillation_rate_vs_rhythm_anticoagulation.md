### Atrial Fibrillation (AF)

**Three management pillars:**

| Pillar | Goal | Tools |
|--------|------|-------|
| Rate | HR <110 (lenient) or <80 (strict) | β-blocker, non-DHP CCB (dilt/verap), digoxin, amiodarone |
| Rhythm | Restore/maintain sinus | Cardioversion (electric / chemical), antiarrhythmics, ablation |
| Stroke prevention | Avoid embolic stroke | CHA₂DS₂-VASc → DOAC / warfarin |

**Hemodynamic stability decides urgency:**

| Stable? | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Unstable (hypotension, ischemia, HF, altered mental status) | **Synchronized DC cardioversion now** |
| Stable | Rate control + assess risk + decide rate vs rhythm strategy |

**CHA₂DS₂-VASc (stroke risk):**

| Letter | Item | Points |
|--------|------|--------|
| C | CHF | 1 |
| H | HTN | 1 |
| A | Age ≥75 | 2 |
| D | DM | 1 |
| S | **Stroke / TIA / thromboembolism** | 2 |
| V | Vascular disease (MI, PAD, plaque) | 1 |
| A | Age 65-74 | 1 |
| Sc | Sex (female) | 1 (only if other RFs present) |

**Anticoagulation thresholds:**

| Score | Recommendation |
|-------|----------------|
| 0 (men) / 1 (women, sex only) | None |
| 1 (men) | Consider |
| ≥2 (men) / ≥3 (women) | Anticoagulate |

**Anticoagulant choice:**

| Drug | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **DOACs** (apixaban, rivaroxaban, dabigatran, edoxaban) | First-line for **non-valvular** AF; less ICH than warfarin |
| Warfarin | Mechanical valve, **moderate-severe mitral stenosis**, severe renal impairment |
| **Mechanical valve / mod-severe MS = warfarin only** | DOACs **contraindicated** |

**HAS-BLED (bleeding risk — used to identify modifiable factors, not to withhold anticoag):**
- Hypertension, Abnormal renal/liver function, Stroke, Bleeding history, Labile INR, Elderly (>65), Drugs/alcohol

**Rate vs rhythm — when to pick which:**

| Favor rhythm | Favor rate |
|--------------|------------|
| Younger, symptomatic, first episode, lone AF | Older, asymptomatic, persistent AF for years |
| HF (early evidence: rhythm > rate) | Comorbidities limit antiarrhythmic |
| Difficult to rate-control | Patient preference, surgical risk |

**Rhythm control modalities:**

| Modality | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Electrical cardioversion | Synchronized DC; requires anticoag |
| Chemical cardioversion | Flecainide, propafenone (no structural disease); amiodarone (any) |
| Catheter ablation | Pulmonary vein isolation; first-line for symptomatic paroxysmal AF in selected pts |

**Cardioversion + anticoagulation rule:**

| Onset | Plan |
|-------|------|
| <48h, low risk | Cardiovert + anticoagulate ≥4 wk after |
| ≥48h or unknown | **3 weeks anticoag before** (or **TEE-guided** to rule out clot) + 4 wk after, then continue per CHA₂DS₂-VASc |

**Rate-control drug choice:**

| Setting | First-line |
|---------|-----------|
| Most patients | β-blocker (metoprolol, atenolol) |
| HFrEF / decompensated | β-blocker (avoid CCB), or digoxin |
| Reactive airway disease | CCB (dilt/verap) preferred |
| Acute on chronic, sedentary | Digoxin (slow onset, narrow window) |

**Watch for AVOID combinations:**
- Non-DHP CCB + β-blocker → bradyarrhythmia
- Non-DHP CCB + HFrEF → decompensation

**Special AF patterns:**

| Pattern | Clue |
|---------|------|
| Paroxysmal | Self-terminates <7 d |
| Persistent | >7 d, requires intervention |
| Long-standing persistent | >12 mo |
| Permanent | Joint decision to stop rhythm pursuit |
| AF with WPW | **Wide, irregular, very fast** — **avoid AV-nodal blockers** (β, CCB, dig, adenosine) → use procainamide or cardiovert |
| Atrial flutter | "Sawtooth" P waves, ~150 bpm with 2:1 block; ablation cure rate high (cavotricuspid isthmus) |

**Common gotchas:**
- **AF + WPW** = no AV-nodal blockers (paradoxically ↑ ventricular rate via accessory pathway)
- **DOACs not for mechanical valve / mod-severe MS** — warfarin only
- Apixaban requires **2 of 3** dose reduction criteria (age ≥80, weight ≤60 kg, Cr ≥1.5)
- Amiodarone **interacts** with warfarin → INR ↑; with digoxin → dig ↑
- Asymptomatic AF still strokes — anticoagulate by score, not symptoms

**Rule of thumb:** Unstable → cardiovert. Otherwise: rate-control, anticoagulate per CHA₂DS₂-VASc (DOAC unless valvular), then choose rate vs rhythm by symptoms/HF status. AF + WPW = no AV-blockers.
