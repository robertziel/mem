### Valvular Heart Disease

**Murmur location + timing summary:**

| Lesion | Murmur | Best heard | Maneuver clues |
|--------|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Aortic stenosis (AS) | Crescendo-decrescendo systolic, ejection | RUSB → carotids | Soft S2, late peak = severe; ↓ with **Valsalva, standing** |
| Aortic regurgitation (AR) | Decrescendo early diastolic | LSB (sit forward, exhale) | ↑ with handgrip; wide pulse pressure, water-hammer pulse |
| Mitral stenosis (MS) | Diastolic rumble + opening snap | Apex (left lateral decubitus) | History of rheumatic fever, A-fib |
| Mitral regurgitation (MR) | Holosystolic | Apex → axilla | ↑ with handgrip; ↓ with Valsalva |
| MVP | Mid-systolic click + late systolic murmur | Apex | Click moves **earlier** with Valsalva (less filling) |
| Tricuspid regurgitation | Holosystolic, LSB | LLSB | ↑ with **inspiration** (Carvallo) |
| HCM (dynamic outflow obstr) | Crescendo-decrescendo systolic | LLSB | ↑ with Valsalva, standing; ↓ with squat (paradoxical to AS) |
| VSD | Holosystolic, harsh | LLSB | ↑ with handgrip |

**Maneuver effects (the trick):**

| Maneuver | Effect on most murmurs | Exceptions ↑ |
|----------|------------------------|--------------|
| Valsalva, standing | ↓ preload → most murmurs ↓ | **HCM, MVP click earlier** |
| Squat, leg-raise, handgrip | ↑ preload/afterload → most murmurs ↑ | HCM ↓, MVP click later |
| Inspiration | ↑ R-sided murmurs | Tricuspid (Carvallo), pulmonic |
| Expiration | ↑ L-sided murmurs | Most |

**Aortic stenosis — severity:**

| Severity | Mean gradient | Valve area | Velocity |
|----------|--------------|-----------|----------|
| Mild | <20 mmHg | >1.5 cm² | <3 m/s |
| Moderate | 20-40 | 1.0-1.5 | 3-4 |
| Severe | >40 | <1.0 | >4 |
| Very severe | >60 | <0.6 | >5 |

**AS classic triad** (severe, symptomatic): SAD = **S**yncope, **A**ngina, **D**yspnea. Once symptomatic → 2-year mortality ~50% without intervention.

**Indications for AS intervention (AVR / TAVR):**

| Class | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| I | Symptomatic severe AS |
| I | Severe AS + EF <50% |
| I | Severe AS undergoing other cardiac surgery |
| IIa | Asymptomatic severe AS with abnormal stress test, very severe (V≥5), rapid progression |

**Mitral stenosis — the rheumatic disease:**

| Cause | Frequency |
|-------|-----------|
| Rheumatic | >90% |
| Other | Calcific (elderly), congenital, SLE |

Severe MS area <1.5 cm². AF + LA enlargement → emboli (use **warfarin**, not DOAC).

**Mitral regurgitation — primary vs secondary:**

| Type | Cause |
|------|-------|
| Primary (organic) | MVP, rheumatic, IE, ruptured chord/papillary |
| Secondary (functional) | LV dilation, ischemic — leaflets normal but annulus stretched |

Treat primary by repairing valve. Secondary improves with HFrEF GDMT first.

**Acute vs chronic MR:**

| | Acute (papillary rupture, IE) | Chronic |
|---|-------------------------------|---------|
| Onset | Sudden | Slow |
| LA size | Normal (no time to dilate) | Dilated |
| Pulm edema | Severe | Late |
| Murmur | Soft (small gradient) | Loud holosystolic |
| Treatment | Emergency surgery + afterload reduction | Repair > replace |

**Aortic regurgitation — eponyms (don't memorize all, recognize the pattern):**

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| Corrigan / water-hammer pulse | Bounding, collapsing |
| de Musset | Head-bobbing |
| Müller | Uvular pulsation |
| Quincke | Capillary pulsation in nail bed |
| Traube | "Pistol shot" femoral |
| Duroziez | Femoral murmur with light pressure |
| Hill sign | LE BP > UE by >20 mmHg |
| Austin Flint | Mid-diastolic rumble at apex (no MS) |

Wide pulse pressure (eg 160/40) is the easy bedside clue.

**Endocarditis prophylaxis (high-risk only):**

| Indications |
|-------------|
| Prosthetic valve / repair material |
| Prior IE |
| Cyanotic congenital heart disease (unrepaired or with residual) |
| Cardiac transplant valvulopathy |

Procedures: **dental** with mucosal trauma, respiratory mucosa procedures, infected skin/MSK. **NOT** for routine GI/GU procedures (since 2007).

**Common gotchas:**
- HCM **vs** AS: Valsalva worsens HCM, improves AS — opposite of intuition
- MVP click moves **earlier** with ↓ preload (less filling → leaflet prolapses sooner)
- MS in AF → **warfarin only** (DOACs contraindicated for moderate-severe MS)
- Acute MR may have **soft murmur** despite severity — high LA pressure equalizes gradient
- AR with rapid progression / dilation → operate before EF drops below 50% or LV end-systolic dimension >50 mm

**Rule of thumb:** Systolic ejection at base = AS. Diastolic blowing at LSB = AR. Apical rumble + opening snap = MS. Holosystolic at apex → axilla = MR. Maneuver-direction reversal = HCM or MVP.
